# Brief: Spoke & Kettle (fictional)

Landing page for **Spoke & Kettle**, a cooperative bicycle repair workshop with a small coffee bar in Rotterdam (Katendrecht). Members get discounted repairs and can use the self-repair benches; anyone can book a repair or buy parts.

Required content:
1. Header: logo/wordmark, nav (Repairs, Shop, Membership, Café, Contact), basket button with item count.
2. Hero with a clear headline, supporting line, and primary + secondary call to action.
3. Repair services: 4 packages (name, what's included, price in EUR, typical turnaround).
4. Repair booking form: name, email, bike type select (city, road, cargo, e-bike), package select, preferred day (next 7 days), time slot (morning/afternoon), submit with inline validation and a success message.
5. Parts & accessories shop: 6 items (name, short note, EUR price, "Add to basket" button that updates the basket count).
6. Membership: 3 tiers (monthly price, perks) with a "Join" button each.
7. Café: opening hours and a short menu (4 items).
8. 3 member testimonials.
9. FAQ accordion (5 questions).
10. Footer with address, hours, social links.

Constraints:
- One self-contained `index.html` (inline CSS and JS). Google Fonts allowed. No external images: use CSS/SVG artwork.
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (basket count, form validation, accordion, mobile navigation).
