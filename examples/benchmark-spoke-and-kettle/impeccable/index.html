      From the benches.

      What members say after a few months of fixing, learning and drinking too much flat white.

      Good questions.

      Anything else, ask at the bar or email hallo@spokeandkettle.nl.

      Do I need to be a member to book a repair?

      No. Anyone can book a repair or buy parts at the listed prices. Members get 20% off repairs and can use the self-repair benches.

      Can I use the benches if I've never fixed a bike?

      Yes. A mechanic is always on the floor during bench hours to show you how, and our monthly evening class covers punctures, brakes and gears from scratch.

      Do you work on e-bikes and cargo bikes?

      We do, including most hub and mid-drive motors. Battery cell repairs we send to a specialist; we'll tell you the cost before anything leaves the building.

      What if my bike needs more than the package?

      We stop and call you first, with a price. Nothing extra gets fitted or charged without your yes.

      How do I cancel or pause my membership?

      Email us or tell us at the counter before the 25th and it stops at the end of that month. Pausing for up to three months is free.
