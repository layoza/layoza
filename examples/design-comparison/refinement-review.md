## Intervals — previous B

**The named weakness improved substantially.** Previously the oversized three-line phone heading and introduction delayed the first photograph. Now the first photograph begins immediately after the compact header; its entire presentation and caption fit before the second work begins. Desktop likewise leads with two large works, moving the introduction below them. Removing the repeated pale external mounts gives the art more direct presence and reduces decorative framing.

All four works, artists, credits, artist information, enquiry form, and local/demo language remain visible in the full phone capture. The first pair are stacked on phone, preserving viewing scale rather than squeezing them into two columns. This is a sensible refinement of the existing light gallery identity.

Tradeoffs: the exhibition title and contextual introduction now arrive after two photographs, so visitors see the work before learning the exhibition premise. The phone header no longer contains a direct artists link, though the section remains. The opening mobile image still has relatively generous side margins. I see no substantial visible content loss or collision.

## Willow — previous C

Tradeoff: the What's on link is absent from the compact phone header, so events have less immediate navigation access. The event section itself remains. No other visible content loss or layout regression stood out.

No visible content loss or major regression stood out.

Screenshots do not establish working controls, search/no-results behavior, date validation, booking confirmation, keyboard access, motion compliance, exact font declarations, or contrast compliance. This review supplies no new functional certification and no comparative superiority claim.
