# Product records — matched fictional studies

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Confirmed by common protocol: local HTML/CSS/JavaScript, no external dependencies.

## Users and purposes
Intervals serves photography enthusiasts: view four historic photographs and make an exhibition enquiry. Willow serves local residents: search four catalogue fixtures by title/author, find opening hours/events and request free membership. Noor serves anxious prospective dental patients in Lahore: understand first visits and treatments and request an appointment. Hearth serves bakery shift leads: review bake quantities, change batch production states, add batches and monitor ovens, stock and collection orders.

## Capabilities and constraints
All four are fictional clients and local demonstrations. No form sends data. No invented claims, staff, addresses, reviews or proof. Shared brief copy and assets are authoritative. Intervals is monochrome, Helvetica only and uncropped. Noor and Intervals have no animation. All controls must work locally and be keyboard-accessible at 1440×1000 and 390×844. Hearth quantities remain visible on phones. No source outside the assigned arm is edited.

## Evidence on hand
The shared protocol, briefs.json, four CC0 Cleveland Museum of Art photographs, one stock library photo, two illustrative stock dental photos and the fixed catalogue/bakery fixtures. No commercial claims or clinical outcome evidence exist.

## Product principles
The artifact leads in a gallery. Search leads in a catalogue. A request is never represented as a booking. A production quantity is never hidden by responsive layout. All copied facts remain truthful.
