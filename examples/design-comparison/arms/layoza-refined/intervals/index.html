Between
the ordinary.

A photographic conversation

A street before the city wakes. People held between departure and arrival. Light caught on the surface of water. Intervals brings photographs by Eugène Atget and Alfred Stieglitz into conversation.

Two ways
of looking.

Eugène Atget 1857–1927

Atget photographed the streets, architecture and gardens of Paris. His patient attention to the everyday created an enduring record of a changing city.

Alfred Stieglitz 1864–1946

A photographer and advocate for photography as an art form, Stieglitz explored modern life through carefully observed scenes and experimental compositions.
