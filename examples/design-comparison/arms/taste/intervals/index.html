A conversation
in photographs.

A street before the city wakes. People held between departure and arrival. Light caught on the surface of water. Intervals brings photographs by Eugène Atget and Alfred Stieglitz into conversation.

Light, held still.

Look a little longer. In these photographs, an ordinary moment becomes something worth returning to.

Two ways
of looking.

Eugène Atget

Atget documented Paris and its surroundings: streets, architecture, gardens and the details of everyday life. His photographs preserve a city in the process of changing.

Alfred Stieglitz

A photographer and advocate for photography as an art, Stieglitz explored modern life through the camera. The Steerage brings people, structure and movement into a single frame.
