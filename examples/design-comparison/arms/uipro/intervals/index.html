Between

Two ways
of seeing.

Eugène Atget

Atget photographed the streets, shopfronts and gardens of Paris. His attentive records turn everyday places into quietly compelling images.

Alfred Stieglitz

Photographer and advocate for photography as an art form, Stieglitz explored the life of the modern city through light, structure and the human figure.
