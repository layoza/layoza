## Composition ideas and selections

PICKED: auto for every brief. These are builder decisions, not user approvals.

### Intervals

1. Museum wall: large asymmetric text heading followed by two full-composition photographic panels, each with its own caption. Later works continue the paired rhythm.
2. Contact-sheet index: all four photographs in a compact, equal gallery grid, catalogue text below.
3. Monograph: one image per viewport, narrow text column and large sequential image sequence.

Picked 1. Makes historical work dominant immediately while allowing the introduction to connect it; alternating vertical offsets create space between two ways of looking. Helvetica alone, monochrome, zero animation. Tradeoff: the long complete page requires scrolling to the third and fourth works. Sections: exhibition introduction, four selected works, two artist biographies, enquiry, credit footer.

### Willow

1. Open-book welcome: serif headline and catalogue search on a warm left page, arch-topped real library photograph to the right; useful hours strip, catalogue, events, joining.
2. Catalogue desk: search and all four book rows open the page, welcome image and events below.
3. Community noticeboard: large event-led weekly programme with the photograph as a wide band and search embedded above it.

Picked 1. Gives residents warmth and a clear search before scrolling. Green/yellow search panel feels related to a library reader card. Tradeoff: less immediate first-screen catalogue detail than 2. Sections: introduction and search, hours, all catalogue fixtures, three events, free member request, fictional-context footer.

### Noor

1. Quiet welcome: Urdu welcome and generous serif headline beside labelled treatment-room image in a tall arch; calm instructional bands below.
2. Conversation first: introduction and appointment request paired at the top, compact image below.
3. First-visit sequence: three explanatory steps dominate the opening; photographic break and request follow.

Picked 1. Creates breathing space for anxious visitors and gives a direct route to a transparent request form, without pressure or fabricated proof. Tradeoff: the illustrative treatment room may feel clinical to some anxious patients. Sections: bilingual welcome, conversational reassurance, first visit, treatment explanations and second illustrative photograph, request, Lahore/demo footer.

### Hearth

1. Day sheet: date, total and state quantities precede an actionable production table; oven/stock sidebar and collections beneath.
2. Baking lanes: queued, baking and ready columns with quantities and actions on every batch.
3. Oven desk: oven timers dominate the top; bake and collection lists below.

Picked 1. Supports a shift lead scanning all products and counts in one consistent ledger, with data updates reflected in summary totals. Mobile rows become two-column record layouts retaining quantities. Tradeoff: no drag interaction from lane approach; every transition has an explicit button. Sections: demo account/date, four operational counts, filters and production table, ovens, stock, collections, demo footer, native add-batch dialog.
