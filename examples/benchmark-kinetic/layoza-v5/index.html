01 / Who we are

Not just another agency. A creative force for what's next.

Torsion is an independent studio of strategists, designers, engineers and motion artists.

We treat every brand as a signal: something that has to travel, hold its shape under pressure and arrive with force. Strategy, design and code sit at one table, so an idea stays intact from first sketch to final frame.

05 / Our process

From possibility to impact.

One continuous line from the first conversation to the work in the world. Each stage builds on the last, nothing gets lost in a hand-off.

  2. 01

    Discover

    We listen first: workshops, audits and audience research until the real problem is on the table.

  3. 02

    Define

    Strategy, positioning and a creative platform sharp enough to say no to the wrong ideas.

  4. 03

    Create

    Identity, product and campaign craft, prototyped in motion from day one rather than bolted on at the end.

  5. 04

    Launch & Evolve

    We ship, measure and keep refining, so the work gains momentum long after launch day.
