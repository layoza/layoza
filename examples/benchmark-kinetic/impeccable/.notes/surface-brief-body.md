# Surface brief: agency homepage (index.html)

Scope: single-file homepage. Visitor mode: Persuade. Brief (BRIEF.md, client prompt) stands in for PRODUCT.md; no interview possible, assumptions labelled below.

Audience: founders and marketing leads choosing a brand/digital studio. Job: feel the studio's craft in one viewport, then act. Action: "Start a Project" reaching a working on-page form (mailto handoff). Proof: no real clients exist, so proof is the page's own craft plus four clearly labelled demonstration projects. No testimonials (omitted per brief).

Assumptions: placeholder wordmark "Name Studio"; email hello@studio.example; social links point to platform roots until real handles exist; essays are drafts, not links; project "View" routes to contact because no case pages exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Kinetic Signal. One sculpted ribbon is the studio's signature and it travels: 3D in the hero, a drawn line through philosophy, process and contact, a fold in every divider. Refuses the split hero + three service cards + logo wall agency template.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian #090B11 and porcelain #F6F5F0 alternate as whole fields; one cobalt #4865FF field (philosophy); lime #D8FF57 only for live states (active index, MOVE's signal, focus, success). Space Grotesk 300-700 display at architectural scale, tight tracking; Inter body; hairline rules #2B303B / #D9DAD6; pill buttons with masked text slide.

STORY: See the studio move (hero), hear its stance (intro), watch each discipline demonstrated (services stage), see demonstration work, feel the seen-to-felt turn, trust the process, play with the reel, read their thinking, start a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7/12: "WE MAKE / IDEAS MOVE." at ~150px on 1440, MOVE outlined-to-solid with a lime signal wave passing through its letters; paragraph and two CTAs below; scroll line bottom-left. Right 5/12 bleeding off-edge: silver-cobalt WebGL ribbon unfolding. Header transparent; "Start a Project" top right.

FORM: pinned by the client brief (Kinetic Signal), position 1 of 1; no concept-seed roll ran, so there is no seed reference.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the ribbon reacting to pointer and scroll (rotates toward centre as the hero hands off), echoed by the draggable ribbon field in the reel.
Motion grammar: masks and clip reveals, expo/power3 decel, one scrubbed timeline per pinned moment, no repeated fade-ups.
