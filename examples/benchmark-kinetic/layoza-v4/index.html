01 / Who we are

Not just another agency. A creative force for what's next.

Obliq is an independent studio of strategists, designers and engineers. We take one sharp idea and turn it into a signal: a brand, a site, a campaign that keeps its shape everywhere it travels.

  • i.One team, strategy to codeThe people who pitch the idea are the people who build it.
  • ii.Prototypes over presentationsWe show ideas moving in the browser, not frozen in slides.
  • iii.Built to keep movingEvery system ships with the rules to grow after launch.

02 / Services

What we do.

Different disciplines. One obsessive standard.

  1. Positioning, naming and identity systems, plus the rules that keep them alive across every touchpoint.

  2. Editorial, high-performance websites designed and engineered in-house, from first wireframe to the last Core Web Vital.

  3. Campaigns, content and media planned around one clear signal, then tuned week by week against what people actually do.

  4. Motion languages, 3D and interactive pieces that make a brand recognisable while it moves.

07 / Insights

Notes from the studio.

Short essays on craft, motion and the decisions behind the work. Open one to read the opening.

  • People recognise a brand by how it behaves long before they can describe how it looks. A logo that arrives the same way every time, a button that answers the same way on every screen: those habits are the identity. We write motion rules into brand guidelines next to colour and type, with timings and curves, so they survive handover.

  • Most visits start on a phone held in one hand. If the name, the promise and the main action do not fit that first screen, no amount of desktop spectacle will rescue the page. We sketch the phone composition first and treat the large canvas as a reward, never as the default.

  • Three equal cards say that every item matters equally, which is rarely true. Real content has a lead, a follow-up and a footnote. Letting the layout follow that hierarchy gives the page rhythm and gives the reader a place to start.
