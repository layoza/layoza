05 / Process

From possibility to impact.

Four stages and one team the whole way through. Nothing is handed over a wall, and every stage leaves you with something you can use.

  1. We listen first: workshops, research, audits and the questions nobody has asked yet.

    You get a shared, written understanding of the problem.

  2. Strategy, positioning and a clear brief. We agree what success looks like before anything is designed.

    You get a strategy and a brief everyone has signed.

  3. Identity, interface, content and motion, made in short loops with you in the room and prototyped in the real medium.

    You get the work itself, tested as it is made.

  4. We ship, measure and keep improving. Launch is the start of the work, not the end of it.

    You get a live product and a plan for what comes next.
