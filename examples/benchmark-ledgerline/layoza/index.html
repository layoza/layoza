Guidance notes

The questions practice owners ask before they connect their first client.

Which banks can Ledgerline read?

Any UK business account that offers an open banking feed. Your client approves read-only access once from their bank's own screens, and can withdraw it whenever they like.

Does Ledgerline file the return for us?

It prepares the nine boxes and sends them under Making Tax Digital only after a named reviewer in your firm approves. Nothing is filed automatically.

What happens to transactions it can't match?

They go to the flag queue with the reason, such as a missing receipt or two possible invoices. Send the client a request link, attach the document yourself, or code the line by hand.

Do our clients need to change how they keep records?

No. They keep sending invoices and receipts the way they do now, by upload, email forwarding or a photo. Ledgerline matches whatever arrives.

You get 30 days with up to 10 clients and every feature of Growing practice. No card is needed, and if you don't continue we delete the trial data.
