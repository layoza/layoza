Your team stays in charge of judgement calls. Ledgerline does the ticking and bashing that used to fill the last week of every quarter.

Security & compliance

Your clients' money data, handled like it matters.

Plain answers to what your clients and your professional body will ask.

Data handling

  • Bank connections are read-only. Ledgerline cannot move money.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Each client's data is kept separate and can be exported or deleted on request.
  • We act as data processor; your firm stays the controller.

Hosting in the UK

  • Application and databases hosted in UK data centres.
  • Backups are stored in the UK.
  • No client data is sent outside the UK for processing.

Access controls

  • Two-factor authentication required for every user.
  • Role-based permissions: limit staff to the clients they work on.
  • Every match, edit, approval and submission is recorded in an audit log.

We'll share our security overview and data processing agreement with any firm evaluating Ledgerline. Ask for it during your trial.
