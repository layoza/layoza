From bank line to filed return in four parts.

Your team stops ticking statements and starts reviewing exceptions. Nothing goes to HMRC until someone in your practice approves it.

  1. 1Connect

    Connect each client’s bank feed

    Read-only Open Banking consent, granted by the client and renewed in one click. Statements can also be imported as CSV or OFX.

  2. 2Match

    Match to invoices and receipts

    Each line is paired with its sales invoice, supplier bill or receipt by amount, date, payee and reference.

  3. 3Review

    Clear the flagged few

    Unmatched lines arrive with a reason. Ask the client for the missing receipt from the same screen.

  4. 4Submit

    Approve and submit

    A named approver signs off, and the return is submitted through Making Tax Digital with the receipt kept on file.
