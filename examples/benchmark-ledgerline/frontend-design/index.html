From bank feed to filed return in four steps

The same order your team already works in, minus the hours of ticking.

  1. 1

    Connect the bank feeds

    Clients approve a read-only connection to their business accounts. New transactions arrive each morning.

  2. 2

    Ledgerline matches

    Each line is checked against sales invoices, supplier bills and receipt photos by amount, date and payee.

  3. 3

    Your team clears the queries

    Anything without a confident match lands in one review list, with the likely candidates beside it.

  4. 4

    Submit the VAT return

    The nine boxes fill from matched lines. A reviewer approves and Ledgerline submits through MTD.

Your clients' bank data stays in the UK

You're trusted with your clients' finances. Here is exactly how we look after them.

Hosting

  • All client data is stored and processed in UK data centres.
  • Backups are kept in a second UK location.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data handling

  • Bank connections are read-only. Ledgerline can never move money.
  • Clients can withdraw bank access at any time.
  • When you leave, we delete your firm's data within 30 days and confirm in writing.

Access controls

  • Two-factor sign-in is required for every staff account.
  • Roles decide who can view, match, approve and submit.
  • Every match, edit and submission is recorded with who did it and when.

Want the detail for your own compliance file? Ask for our data processing agreement and security questionnaire answers at security@ledgerline.example.
