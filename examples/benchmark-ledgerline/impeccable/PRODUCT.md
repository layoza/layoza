## Users

Practice owners and practice managers at small and mid-size UK accountancy firms (3 to 50 staff). They evaluate tools for their whole practice, think in clients, hours, and VAT quarters, and are accountable for MTD compliance.

## Product Purpose

Ledgerline (fictional) reads clients' bank feeds, matches transactions to invoices and receipts, flags the ones it cannot match, and prepares VAT returns for Making Tax Digital submission. Success: fewer staff hours spent on bookkeeping reconciliation and a calmer VAT deadline.

## Positioning

(inferred) The mechanism is the exception queue: the software clears the matchable lines and hands the accountant only the lines it could not match, so human time goes to judgement, not ticking.

## Operating Context
