Client money data, handled like client money

What happens to your clients' bank data, where it lives, and who in your practice can see it.

Data handling
  • Bank access is read-only. Ledgerline can see transactions; it cannot move money.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • You can export or delete a client's data at any time, and it is removed when you close an account.
Hosting region
  • Client data is stored and processed in UK data centres.
  • Backups stay in the UK region too.
Access controls
  • Roles for staff, reviewers and partners; only partners approve VAT returns.
  • Restrict any client to named members of your team.
  • Two-step sign-in for every user, and an audit log of who viewed or changed what.

Questions practices ask first

What happens to transactions Ledgerline can't match?

They wait in that client's exception queue with the closest candidates beside them. A member of your team picks the right document, splits the line, or marks it as needing the client. Nothing unmatched is ever forced into the VAT return.

Does Ledgerline submit VAT returns to HMRC by itself?

No. It prepares the return for Making Tax Digital and shows the lines behind each box. A partner, or whoever you give approval rights to, reviews and approves it before it is submitted.

How do clients connect their bank accounts?

You send the client an invitation. They choose their bank and approve read-only access in their own banking app. You see the feed as soon as they approve; you never handle their banking credentials.

Can we try it on our real clients during the trial?

Yes. Connect as many clients as your plan size allows, run a full VAT quarter through it, and keep everything if you subscribe. If you don't, the data is deleted at the end of the trial.

Where is our clients' data stored?

In UK data centres, including backups. See the security section above for how access is controlled inside your practice.
