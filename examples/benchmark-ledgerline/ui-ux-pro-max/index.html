How it works

From bank feed to filed VAT return in four steps

Ledgerline does the repetitive matching. Your team spends its time on the transactions that actually need a person.

  1. Step 01

    Connect bank feeds

    Each client authorises read-only access to their business accounts. New transactions arrive daily.

  2. Step 02

    Match automatically

    Transactions are matched to invoices, bills and receipts by amount, date, payee and reference.

  3. Step 03

    Review the exceptions

    Anything unmatched lands in one queue, with a suggested fix or a one-click request to the client.

  4. Step 04

    Approve and submit

    The nine-box VAT return is prepared from reconciled data. A partner signs off before it goes through MTD.

Security & compliance

Your clients' financial data, handled carefully

Here is exactly how we look after it: where it lives, who can see it, and what happens when you leave.

Hosted in the UK

Production data and backups are stored in UK data centres. Data is not moved outside the UK for processing.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest with AES-256, including backups.

Read-only bank access

Bank connections can view transactions only. Ledgerline cannot move money, and clients can revoke consent at any time.

Role-based access

Give each person only the clients and actions they need. Two-factor sign-in is required for every user.

Audit trail

Every match, edit, approval and submission is logged with who did it and when.

Data retention & exit

Export all your data at any time. When you close your account, we delete it on a schedule you can see.

UK GDPR

We act as your data processor under a written data processing agreement you can review before signing up.

We don't list certifications we don't hold. If your firm has a security questionnaire, send it to us and we'll answer it in full.
