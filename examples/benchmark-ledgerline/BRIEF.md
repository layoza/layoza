# Brief: Ledgerline (fictional)

Landing page for **Ledgerline**, a B2B software product for small and mid-size UK accountancy firms. It reads clients' bank feeds, matches transactions to invoices and receipts, flags the ones it can't match, and prepares VAT returns for Making Tax Digital submission. Firms of 3–50 staff; buyers are practice owners and practice managers.

Required content:
1. Header: logo/wordmark, nav (Product, How it works, Pricing, Security, Customers), "Sign in" link and "Start free trial" button.
2. Hero with a clear headline, supporting line, primary + secondary call to action, and a visual that shows what the product actually does.
3. How it works: 3–4 steps from bank feed to filed VAT return.
4. Feature section: 6 capabilities (title + one-line explanation).
5. Interactive element: a savings estimator — inputs for number of clients and average transactions per client per month; shows estimated hours saved per month (assume 40 seconds saved per transaction) and updates live.
6. Pricing: 3 plans (monthly GBP price per firm, client limit, features) with a monthly/yearly toggle (yearly = 2 months free) and a CTA each.
7. Security & compliance: data handling, hosting region (UK), access controls. No invented certifications.
8. 3 customer quotes from fictional firms.
9. FAQ accordion (5 questions).
10. Trial signup form: name, work email, firm name, firm size select (1–2, 3–10, 11–50), submit with inline validation and a success message.
11. Footer with company address, links, social links.

Constraints:
- One `index.html` with inline CSS and JS. Google Fonts allowed. Pinned-version JavaScript libraries from cdn.jsdelivr.net are allowed. No external images: use CSS/SVG/canvas artwork.
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (estimator, pricing toggle, form validation, accordion, mobile navigation).
- No invented awards, certifications, customer logos of real companies, or real-world claims.
