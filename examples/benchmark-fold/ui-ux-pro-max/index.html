02 — Approach

Four things we hold to on every job, whatever its size.

  1. 01

    Start with what is there

    We survey before we sketch. The most useful building is usually the one already standing.

  2. 02

    Add as little as possible

    Every new element has to earn its place. Fewer, better parts are easier to build and to keep.

  3. 03

    Timber first

    We reach for timber and other low-carbon materials, detailed so they can be repaired rather than replaced.

  4. 04

    Draw it with the people who use it

    Residents, staff and neighbours see the drawings early, while changes are still cheap.

03 — Studio

Six people, one room, and a large table for drawings.

Fold is a small practice by choice. Every project is led by an architect who stays with it from the first visit to the last snag list.

We work from a ground-floor studio on the south side of the city, mostly for housing associations, community groups and households. We are happy to take on small jobs, and to tell you honestly when you may not need an architect at all.
