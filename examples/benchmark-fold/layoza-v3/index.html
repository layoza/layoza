Studio

Fold is six people in one room in the Merchant City. Everyone draws, everyone makes models, and everyone goes to site. We stay small so the person you meet at the first conversation is the person who sees your building finished.

Approach

  1. Keep what is already there.

    The lowest-carbon building is usually the one standing. We repair stone, slate and sash before we replace anything, and we survey the existing building first so the new work can stay modest.

  2. Make it warm, and let it breathe.

    Old walls need to dry out. We insulate tenements and cottages with breathable materials such as wood fibre, lime and cork, and we draw every junction where damp could gather.

  3. Build the new rooms in timber.

    When a building needs to grow, we frame and clad it in timber: light on old foundations, quick to put up, and quieter for the people next door.

  4. Design it with the people who use it.

    Every project starts at a table with a card model you can pick up and change. Residents, committees and neighbours see the building at 1:100 before anyone signs a drawing.
