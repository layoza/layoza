Approach

  • Keep what is there

    The greenest building is usually the one already standing. We start by surveying what can be repaired before drawing anything new.

  • Build in timber where it suits

    Timber is light enough to sit on old foundations and quick to put up on tight sites. Where it does not suit, we say so.

  • Draw it with the people who use it

    Residents, committees and families see the drawings early and often, at a scale they can read and mark up.

  • Plan for the next repair

    We choose materials and details that a local tradesperson can maintain, and leave a clear record of what was done.
