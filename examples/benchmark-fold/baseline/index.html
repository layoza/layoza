Approach

Four things we hold to on every job, whatever the size.

  1. 01

    Keep what is there

    The greenest building is usually the one already standing. We start by asking what can stay.

  2. 02

    Fabric first

    Walls, roofs and windows before gadgets. A well-insulated, airtight building needs less of everything else.

  3. 03

    Build in timber

    Where we add, we use wood: light on old foundations, quick to build, and pleasant to live with.

  4. 04

    Design with the people who stay

    Residents, staff and neighbours know a building best. We draw with them, not just for them.

Studio
