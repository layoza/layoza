Approach

A-300 Wall build-up, outside to in
  1. 1

    Keep what is there

    Stone, slate and old timber are already paid for in carbon and money. Every project starts with a measured survey, not a demolition plan.

    Layer 1: existing stone
  2. 2

    Let old walls breathe

    Solid walls need to dry out. We use vapour-open insulation and detail every junction so moisture has somewhere to go.

    Layer 2: wood-fibre lining
  3. 3

    Build new in timber

    Where something new is needed, it is timber wherever it can be: light on old foundations and quick and quiet to put up.

    Layer 3: battens and services
  4. 4

    Plan for the people inside

    Retrofits happen around lives. We phase the work so residents, staff and hall users can stay while it happens.

    Layer 4: the room

Studio

A-400 Studio plan

Six people around one long table in the West End. We are small on purpose: the person who draws your building is the person who visits the site.

Fold works with householders, housing associations and community groups. We take on a handful of projects a year and see each one through from first survey to the last snagging visit.
