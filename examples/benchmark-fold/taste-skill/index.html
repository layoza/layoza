Approach

Four rules we work to on every job, from a single sash window to a new public building.

  • Keep what is there

    Most of the carbon in a building was spent the day it was built. Every job starts with a survey of what can stay, and the design works around it.

  • Draw the new work in orange

    Every drawing we issue shows the existing building in grey and our work in one colour, so owners, builders and planners can see exactly what changes.

  • Build in timber where it fits

    Timber frames and cross-laminated panels are light enough for back courts and old floors, and quick to put up when neighbours are living through the work.

  • Stay until it works

    The architect who drew the project visits site every week until handover, and comes back after a year to see how the building is being used.
