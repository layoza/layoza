# Product

> All facts below are INFERRED from the client brief (BRIEF.md). No answer mechanism was available (unattended benchmark run, no question tool); assumptions are labelled.

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: single static index.html with inline CSS and JS (pinned by the brief); Google Fonts allowed.

## Users
Prospective clients of a small architecture studio: homeowners planning a retrofit or timber extension, housing associations and community/civic groups commissioning small buildings (inferred from the work types). They visit to judge whether Fold is the right fit and then send an enquiry.

## Product Purpose
Fold (fictional) is a 6-person architecture studio in Glasgow working on housing retrofits, small civic buildings and timber extensions. The site shows the work, explains the approach, introduces the people, and collects project enquiries.

## Positioning
Small, Glasgow-based, focused on retrofit and modest timber/civic work rather than large new-build. (Inferred; no further differentiators supplied.)

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Required: header with wordmark + nav (Work, Studio, Approach, Contact) + mobile nav; hero statement + link to work; 6 projects filterable by type (All, Housing, Civic, Extensions); 4 principles; 6 people + paragraph; enquiry form (name, email, project type, budget range, message) with inline validation and success message; footer with address, email, phone, social links.
- No external images; CSS/SVG artwork only.
- Must work at 1440x900, 768x1024, 375x812.

## Brand Commitments
Client-pinned style: simple, quiet and minimal. No animation beyond basic hover/focus states. Restraint is the point, but it must feel authored, not empty or generic.

## Evidence on Hand
None. No real projects, people, address, awards or press. All project names, people, contact details are illustrative placeholders and must not be presented as real-world claims. No awards, client logos, testimonials or metrics.

## Product Principles
1. Restraint is the brand: every element must earn its place.
2. Show the buildings (drawings) before talking about them.
3. Make enquiry easy and honest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA as a working target (inferred default).
