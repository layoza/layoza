# Surface brief: Fold landing page (index.html)

Scope: single landing page, visitor mode Persuade. Audience: homeowners, housing associations and community groups weighing a small Glasgow practice for a retrofit, small civic building or timber extension. Action: send an enquiry (working form on the page); secondary: browse the work.
Constraints (client-pinned): simple, quiet, minimal; no animation beyond basic hover/focus; CSS/SVG artwork only; single index.html.
Run notes: unattended benchmark run, no question tool or decision page answer possible; assigned direction built without a decision round; no image generation, so code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the studio's model table: white card and greyboard study models, drawn in axonometric, are the only imagery. It refuses the category default of a full-bleed photo hero and a masonry grid of project shots, and its predictable opposite (concrete brutalism).

OWN-WORLD: Mountboard white ground #F3F3F0, card white faces, greyboard #B9B4AB / #8C877E shadow faces, balsa #D6BF94 for timber parts, ink #1D1C1A, one cutting-mat green #1F5A4A reserved strictly for things you can act on (links, buttons, active filter, focus). Archivo with its width axis: expanded light display, normal-width body. Hairline 1px ink rules; solid line = cut, dashed line = fold, in every drawing.

STORY: Visitor learns what Fold builds in one sentence, sees six models they can filter by type, reads four ordered working principles, meets six people at the studio table, and enquires.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (folded-card wordmark left, four links right). Statement in expanded light Archivo across ~9 of 12 columns at ~4.25rem, one green "See the work" link below. Beneath it, full content width, the hero study model (tenement with balsa rear extension) on a greyboard plinth with a scale bar and drawing caption; nothing boxed.

FIRST VIEWPORT ADAPTATION (after finish review): at desktop widths the statement takes columns 1-6 at up to 3.75rem with the model beside it on columns 6-12, so the whole study model sits inside the 1440x900 fold; at tablet and phone the statement sits above the full-width model as written. Reason: a full-width model under a 9-column statement pushed the model below the fold.

FORM: Candidate 5 of 7 (studio model table / card study models). seed: none (unavailable) (roll printed key 8a34af42, no SEED REFERENCE line; `concept-seed --check-seed none` exited 0)
Raises: from Miura sheet (declined): one crease grammar, cut solid / fold dashed, across all artwork. From automata (declined): every model captioned on a strip beneath it. From warm consumer app (declined): action colour only on actionable things. From darkroom (declined): approach told as ordered stations of real work. From WPA poster (declined): one plain heading per section, no eyebrows. From algorave (declined): state visible before it acts (filter counts, field errors shown on blur).
Signature: the filterable model table; the studio plan with six seats.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
