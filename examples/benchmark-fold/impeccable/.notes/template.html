Approach

Every project moves through the same four steps, in this order. The drawings follow one wall through all of them.

  1. {{sec1}}

    1Measure what is there

    We survey before we draw: walls, damp, daylight, and how the rooms are really used.

  2. {{sec2}}

    2Keep what still works

    Stone, timber and plaster that still do their job stay. Most of a building’s carbon is already spent.

  3. {{sec3}}

    3Make it warm and dry

    Insulation, airtightness and ventilation come first, in that order, before any new heating kit.

  4. {{sec4}}

    4Add only what is needed

    When a building needs more room, we add it in timber, at the scale of the street around it.
