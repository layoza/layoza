# Brief: Fold Architects (fictional) — style: SIMPLE

Landing page for **Fold**, a 6-person architecture studio in Glasgow working on housing retrofits, small civic buildings and timber extensions.

**Style direction from the client (must be followed): simple, quiet and minimal. No animation beyond basic hover/focus states. Restraint is the point — but it must still feel authored, not empty or generic.**

Required content:
1. Header: wordmark, nav (Work, Studio, Approach, Contact), mobile navigation.
2. Hero: one clear statement of what the studio does, plus a link to the work.
3. Selected work: 6 projects (name, place, year, type, one-line description), filterable by type (All, Housing, Civic, Extensions).
4. Approach: 4 principles.
5. Studio: 6 people (name, role) and a short paragraph.
6. Contact/enquiry form: name, email, project type select, budget range select, message; inline validation and a success message.
7. Footer with address, email, phone, social links.

Constraints:
- One `index.html` with inline CSS and JS. Google Fonts allowed. No external images: use CSS/SVG artwork (drawings, plans, sections are fine).
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (project filter, form validation, mobile navigation).
- No invented awards or real-world claims.
