Questions

Anything else, email hello@volta.example.

How far will 80 km really get me?

80 km is the most you’ll get from one charge in the lowest assist level on mostly flat roads. In the highest assist level on steep hills, plan for around half that.

How do I charge a battery that’s inside the frame?

Plug the charger into the port on the down tube. Or unlock the battery with the key, slide it out from below and charge it indoors. A full charge takes about 4 hours.

Does the belt need looking after?

No oil and no degreaser. Rinse off grit after muddy rides. We check the belt tension at the yearly service.

Which size should I choose?

S fits riders from 155 to 170 cm, M from 168 to 183 cm and L from 180 to 195 cm. If you’re between sizes, book a test ride and try both.

Can I try one before I buy?

Yes. We run test rides in Bristol, Bath and Cardiff. Book a test ride and we’ll meet you with a bike in your size.
