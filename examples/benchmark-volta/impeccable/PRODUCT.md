## Users

City commuters and errand riders in and around Bristol, Bath and Cardiff weighing a first or replacement e-bike. [inferred from the test-ride cities] They arrive to judge whether a light, low-maintenance e-bike is worth £2,190, and leave either booking a test ride or ordering.

## Product Purpose

Volta One (fictional) is a lightweight electric city bike from a small Bristol company. Success for the page: a visitor understands the three mechanisms (battery hidden in the frame, belt drive, 16 kg weight), picks a colour, and books a test ride or orders.

## Positioning

A city e-bike light enough to carry upstairs (16 kg) with the battery hidden inside the frame and a belt instead of a chain, made by a small Bristol company. [inferred framing of the brief's facts]

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 16 kg, 80 km range, belt drive, battery hidden in the frame.
- Colours: Graphite, Moss, Clay. Price £2,190.
- Test rides offered in Bristol, Bath, Cardiff.
- Undecided: motor power, gearing, charge time, tyre size, frame sizes. Any spec beyond the brief's facts shown on the page is illustrative and must be confirmed by the client.

## Brand Commitments

- Style direction pinned by the client: a real-time 3D experience; the bike or its parts in interactive, rotatable 3D tied to the scroll story; procedural geometry only, no model files; graceful degradation without WebGL and with reduced motion.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real testimonials, press, awards, reviews, ratings or certifications exist. Rider quotes are fictional and must be presented as such. No awards or real-world claims may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the mechanism, not the adjective: every claim is a part you can see and turn.
2. Light is the product: the page should feel as unburdened as 16 kg.
3. Local and small: Bristol makers, not a global brand voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target; 3D must never be the only carrier of information; reduced motion and no-WebGL are first-class states (brief requirement).
