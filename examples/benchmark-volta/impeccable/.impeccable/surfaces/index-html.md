## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the box the bike arrives in. Not a dark studio product stage with glowing edges; a corrugated kraft carton printed in one flexo ink, opened, with the real bike inside that you can turn.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft board ground (#C79F6E family), lighter liner inside, one deep flexo blue ink used solid with kraft knock-outs and as line print. Big Shoulders Stencil for printed display, Archivo for set text. Die-lines (dashed cut, dotted fold), handling marks (this-way-up arrows, a printed 16 KG weight mark), tick-box variant fields, the sliced corrugated edge (sine flute between two liners) as the section seam. Bike colours (Graphite, Moss, Clay) appear only on the product.

STORY: Visitor sees the bike in the open box, turns it, scrolls: battery x-rays out of the down tube, the belt runs, the bike lifts at 16 kg; ticks a colour; reads the spec panel; fills the shipping-label booking form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed liner stage; the VOLTA brand printed large in screened ink behind the 3D bike, bike centre-right at ~60% width; shipping label at left carrying the h1 Volta One, promise, £2,190, Order (solid ink) and Book a test ride; header as the printed top flap strip; rotate controls printed as handling marks.

FORM: grounded candidate 5 of 7, the shipping carton. seed: none (unavailable). The roll printed no SEED REFERENCE line; its key a0ee01e8 is in the roll output only.

Signature interaction: drag-to-turn 3D bike whose scroll-driven chapters (battery, belt, weight, colour) re-stage the same object along one camera axis. Motion grammar: eased dolly + part reveals, snapping under reduced motion; SVG side elevation when WebGL is absent.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
