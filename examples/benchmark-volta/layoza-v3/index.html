Questions at the door.

Ask us anything else when you come for a test ride.

How far does it go on one charge?

Up to 80 km. Hills, headwinds, a heavy bag and the highest assist level all bring that down, so most riders plan on a charge or two a week.

How do I charge a battery I can’t see?

Plug the charger into the port on the down tube, just above the pedals. A full charge takes about four hours, so overnight in the hall is plenty.

Does the belt need looking after?

Very little. It needs no oil. Rinse off grit after a muddy ride and we check its tension at each service.

Which size should I ride?

The frame comes in S, M and L. Book a test ride and we bring two sizes so you can feel the difference before you choose.

Can I ride it in the rain?

Yes. The battery is sealed inside the frame, the mudguards come fitted and the belt does not rust. Dry it off when you get in and it is ready for tomorrow.
