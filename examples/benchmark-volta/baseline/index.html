FAQ

Questions.

Anything else, email the workshop and a person will reply.

How far will it really go on one charge?

Up to 80 km in mixed assist. Riding mostly in the highest assist level, up steep hills or into a headwind will bring that down. The display shows remaining range based on how you are actually riding.

Can I take the battery inside to charge?

Yes. Unlock it with the key and it slides out of the bottom of the down tube. You can also plug the charger straight into the frame.

Does a belt drive need special maintenance?

Very little. There is no oil; rinse it with water when it gets gritty. Belt tension is checked at your first service.

Which size should I order?

As a guide: S for riders around 160–172 cm, M for 170–184 cm, L for 182–195 cm. If you are between sizes, book a test ride and try both.

How does delivery work?

Delivery is free to UK mainland addresses. The bike arrives fully built and checked; you just set the saddle height and charge the battery.
