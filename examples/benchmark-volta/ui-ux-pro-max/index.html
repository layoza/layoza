01 / 03

The battery lives inside the frame.

The 360 Wh pack slides into the down tube, so there is no box bolted to the outside and the bike keeps a clean line. It unlocks with a key and comes out for charging indoors.

80 kmrange on a charge, depending on rider, route and assist level

02 / 03

A belt, not a chain.

A carbon-reinforced belt runs from the front ring to the rear cog. Nothing to oil, nothing to mark your trousers, and it stays quiet in the rain.

0chain oil, ever

03 / 03

Light enough to carry upstairs.

Hiding the battery and dropping the chain let us keep the frame simple. At 16 kg you can lift it onto a train, up a stairwell or into a hallway hook.
