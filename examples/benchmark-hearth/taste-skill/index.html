Morning bakery

Hearth rye, £6.50

Counter open Tuesday to Sunday from 8:00 until the bread runs out.

One fire, lit at half four

Morning, the falling heat

The fire burns for ninety minutes until the floor reads 300°C. Then it is raked out and the bread goes straight onto the bricks: rye first, sourdough next, buns and spelt as the heat drops. By eleven the oven is a slow cooker for beans and stocks.

Evening, a new fire

At three the fire is rebuilt at the back. By half five the dome is at 380°C and the whole menu comes out of it, in view of all 40 seats. When the last plate goes, tomorrow's beans go in.

    Questions

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Yes. Twelve of our 40 seats are at the counter facing the kitchen, and we keep them for walk-ins every night we're open. The tables are booked online up to 30 days ahead.

    Can you cook for allergies and coeliacs?

    Tell us in your booking notes and we'll plan around it. The dishes marked GF contain no gluten, but flour is in the air from 6:00 and everything shares one oven, so we can't promise a kitchen free of traces.

    Can I reserve a loaf?

    Yes. Call or email by 18:00 the day before and we'll hold it at the counter until 11:00. Saturday rye usually goes first, so that's the one to ask for.

    Is it step-free, and are children welcome?

    The entrance on Tib Street is level, and there's an accessible toilet. Children are welcome at dinner before 19:30 and at the bakery counter any time; we have two high chairs.

    Do larger tables pay a deposit?

    Tables of five to eight leave £10 a head, which comes off the bill. Cancel up to 48 hours before and it's returned in full.
