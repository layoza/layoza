One oven, one day

There is no second oven and no gas back-up. Everything on this page is cooked by one fire as it climbs, falls and climbs again. Drag through the day to see what's inside.

The baker lights the fire at half past four. By six the floor of the oven is hot enough to set a crust in minutes, so the loaves go in first. As the heat drops, it suits the buns and pastry, and later the pots of beans and stock that will be part of tonight's dinner.

In the afternoon the cooks rake out the ash and build the fire back up. By half past five it is at its hottest, and dinner is cooked at the mouth where you can watch it from the counter. After the last table, a lamb shoulder goes into the cooling oven and stays there until morning, when the baker lights the fire again.

Before you come

Anything else, call us on 0161 496 0732 between 10:00 and 16:00.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. The ten counter seats facing the oven are kept for walk-ins every night. Come early in the evening for the best chance; we'll take your number if there's a wait.

Can you cook for allergies?

Tell us when you book and again when you arrive. Most dishes can be adjusted. We bake bread every morning, so flour is in the air and we can't guarantee anything is free from traces of gluten.

Can I reserve bread from the bakery?

Ask at the counter the day before and we'll put your loaf aside until 12:00. We don't take bread orders online or by phone.

Is the restaurant step-free?

The entrance and dining room are on one level and there is an accessible toilet. Counter stools are high; if you'd rather sit at a table, mention it when you book.

Are children welcome?

Yes, at tables rather than the counter, which sits close to the fire. We have two high chairs; let us know if you need one.
