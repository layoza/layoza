# Brief: Hearth & Rye (fictional) — style: client wants "the best restaurant website in the world"

Landing page for **Hearth & Rye**, a 40-cover wood-fired restaurant and morning bakery in Manchester's Northern Quarter. Bread is baked from 6am in the same oven that cooks the evening menu; the kitchen is open to the room. Dinner Wednesday–Sunday, bakery counter Tuesday–Sunday mornings.

Required content:
1. Header: wordmark, nav (Menu, Bakery, Book, Private dining, Visit), "Book a table" button, mobile navigation.
2. Hero with a clear headline, supporting line, primary + secondary call to action.
3. Tonight's menu: 4 small plates, 4 large plates, 3 desserts (name, short description, GBP price, dietary tags V/VG/GF), with a filter by dietary tag.
4. Morning bakery: 6 breads/pastries with prices and "sold out by" times.
5. Table booking form: name, email, party size (1–8), date (next 30 days, Wed–Sun only), time (17:30–21:30 every 30 min), dietary notes; inline validation and a success message.
6. Private dining: the chef's table for up to 10, what's included, enquiry button.
7. The oven: a short story of how one oven runs the whole day.
8. 3 guest reviews (fictional).
9. FAQ accordion (5 questions).
10. Footer with address, opening hours, phone, social links.

Constraints:
- One `index.html` with inline CSS and JS. Google Fonts allowed. Pinned-version JavaScript libraries from cdn.jsdelivr.net are allowed. No external images: use CSS/SVG/canvas artwork.
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (dietary filter, booking validation, accordion, mobile navigation).
- No invented awards, press quotes or real-world claims.
