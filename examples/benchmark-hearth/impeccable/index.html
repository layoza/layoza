One oven. Bread at six. Fire till late.

Hearth & Rye is a 40-cover wood-fired restaurant and morning bakery in Manchester's Northern Quarter. The loaves you buy at breakfast come out of the same oven that cooks your dinner, and the kitchen is open to the room.
