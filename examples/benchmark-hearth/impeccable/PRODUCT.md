## Product Purpose

Hearth & Rye is a 40-cover wood-fired restaurant and morning bakery. The site must get visitors to book a table, plan a bakery visit before things sell out, and enquire about the chef's table. Success = bookings and enquiries submitted.

## Positioning

One wood-fired oven runs the whole day: bread from 6am, then the evening menu, in the same oven, with the kitchen open to the room. No neighbouring restaurant can truthfully copy that single-oven day.

## Operating Context

- Dinner Wednesday–Sunday; bakery counter Tuesday–Sunday mornings.
- Booking slots 17:30–21:30 every 30 minutes, party size 1–8, next 30 days, Wed–Sun only.
- Chef's table seats up to 10.
- Location: Manchester, Northern Quarter. Exact street address, phone and social handles are not given (inferred placeholders, must be replaced).

## Product Principles

1. The oven is the protagonist; everything traces back to one fire across one day.
2. Booking is the primary action and must always be one tap away.
3. Time matters: dinner slots, bakery sell-out times, opening days are shown plainly.
4. Honesty over hype: no invented accolades.
