    The oven

    One fire, the whole day.

    We don’t have a gas range or a back-up oven. Everything on this page comes out of one brick dome, and the menu follows its heat.

    The fire is fierce at dawn, which is when bread wants it. As it falls, pastries go in, then pots that need hours. By evening we build it back up for the dinner menu and cook in front of you.

    1. 05:30

      The fire is lit

      Seasoned hardwood goes in on the left of the floor. In about half an hour the soot on the dome burns off to clean, pale brick: the sign it’s hot enough.

    2. 06:00

      Bread, in the fiercest heat

      The embers are swept aside and the sourdough goes straight onto the bricks. The first loaves are out and cooling before the street wakes up.

    3. 10:00

      Pastries in the falling heat

      As the oven eases off, croissants and cardamom knots bake gently enough not to scorch.

    4. 14:00

      Pots for the evening

      Beans, stocks and braises sit in the retained heat for hours. Nothing is wasted: the morning’s fire becomes the evening’s depth.

    5. 17:30

      Built back up for dinner

      Fresh wood, a live flame, and the kitchen faces the room. You’ll see your dinner go in, and come out.

    6. 23:00

      Embers banked

      The fire is raked back and left to cool, ready to be lit again in a few hours.
