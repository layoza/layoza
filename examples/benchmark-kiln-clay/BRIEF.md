# Brief: Kiln & Clay (fictional)

Landing page for **Kiln & Clay**, a small-batch ceramics studio in Lahore that sells handmade tableware online and runs weekend wheel-throwing workshops.

Required content:
1. Header: logo/wordmark, nav (Shop, Workshops, Studio, Journal, Contact), cart button with item count.
2. Hero with a clear headline, supporting line, and primary + secondary call to action.
3. Featured collection: 6 products (name, glaze/colour note, price in PKR, "Add to cart" button that updates the cart count).
4. Workshops: 3 upcoming sessions (date, level, seats left) and a booking form (name, email, session select, number of participants 1-4, submit with inline validation and a success message).
5. Studio story section.
6. 3 testimonials.
7. FAQ accordion (5 questions).
8. Newsletter signup.
9. Footer with address, hours, social links.

Constraints:
- One self-contained `index.html` (inline CSS and JS). Google Fonts allowed. No external images: use CSS/SVG artwork.
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (cart count, form validation, accordion, mobile navigation).
