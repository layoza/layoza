    03 / Studio

    A garage in Gulberg, a gas kiln, and too much clay

    Kiln & Clay started in 2019, when potter Ayesha Rana moved her wheel into the garage behind her family’s house on Main Boulevard and fired a first, mostly cracked, load of bowls.

    We blend fine river silt dug near the Ravi with a stoneware body so that pieces survive daily use: chai at 7 am, daal at 9 pm, the dishwasher in between. Our glazes are mixed in-house. The blues come from the kashi tiles on the Wazir Khan Mosque and the old city gates, the greens from the Shalimar gardens, the ochre from haldi stains on every Lahori kitchen counter.

    Everything is thrown, trimmed and glazed by two people, which is why batches are small and why we close every Monday to unload the kiln.
