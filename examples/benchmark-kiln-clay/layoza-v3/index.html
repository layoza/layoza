Three wheels, one gas kiln, a lane off Main Boulevard

Kiln & Clay began in 2019 in a garage in Gulberg, after our founder Hira Qureshi spent two winters learning from a family of potters in Multan, where blue-and-white is a way of life rather than a trend.

We throw a dense stoneware body and dip-glaze in colours we know from home: the cobalt and turquoise of the Wazir Khan tile panels, the ochre of turmeric, the green of Shalimar’s water channels. Every glaze is mixed in the studio and tested on a tile before it touches a plate.

We make in small batches, about one firing a fortnight, because a kiln this size rewards patience. When a batch sells out, it’s gone until the next opening.
