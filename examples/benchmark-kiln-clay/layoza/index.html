    03The studioGulberg III, since 2017

    One kiln, four wheels, and a lot of chai

    Kiln & Clay began in a garage behind our family home in Gulberg, with a second-hand wheel and a kiln we rebuilt brick by brick.

    Today we are a team of five potters. We dig part of our clay from the banks near Shahdara, blend it with stoneware for strength, and mix every glaze ourselves, many inspired by the blue-white tiles of Multan and the copper reds of old Lahori pottery.

    We fire roughly once a week. When a batch sells out, it is gone; the next one will be a little different. That is the point.
