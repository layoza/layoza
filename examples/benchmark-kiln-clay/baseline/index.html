The studio

A garage, a kick wheel, and a stubborn idea.

Kiln & Clay began in 2019 when Ayesha Rana bought a second-hand kick wheel from a potter retiring in Gujrat and set it up in her parents’ garage in Gulberg.

She wanted the plates on Lahore’s tables to carry the city’s own colours — Multani blue, Shalimar green, the rust of old Anarkali brick. Today the studio is four potters, one gas kiln and a long teak table where workshop students drink chai between throws.

We dig and blend our stoneware body from Punjab river clays, mix every glaze in-house, and fire no more than forty pieces at a time, so each one is checked by hand before it leaves.

  • ThrowCentred, opened and pulled on the wheel.
  • TrimFeet carved at leather-hard, then bisqued.
  • FireGlazed, then twelve hours to 1,240°C.

— Ayesha & the studio team
