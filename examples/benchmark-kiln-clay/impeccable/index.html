    You’re on the wheel.

    One kiln, two wheels and a courtyard in Gulberg.

    Kiln & Clay began in 2019 as a shared studio for two potters trained between Multan’s blue-pottery workshops and a ceramics degree at NCA.

    We mix our own stoneware body from Punjab clays, throw every form on the wheel, and glaze in colours lifted from the kashi tilework a short drive away at Wazir Khan. We fire roughly every three weeks, and each batch goes on this page the morning the kiln is cool enough to open.

    Nothing is slip-cast and nothing is outsourced. If a piece cracks, warps or crawls in the kiln, it never reaches you.
