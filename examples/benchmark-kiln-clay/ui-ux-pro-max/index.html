      Our studio

      A converted haveli garage, two kilns, and a lot of patience.

      Kiln & Clay began in 2017 when Mahnoor Rafiq traded her architecture desk for a second-hand wheel in a Gulberg garage. Today a team of four potters works with Punjab river clays and stoneware bodies, mixing every glaze in-house from tested, lead-free recipes.

      We fire slowly — a full day up to temperature and two days to cool — because rushed kilns make brittle pots. It means smaller batches, and it means the mug in your hand will outlast the kitchen it lives in.

      “Clay remembers everything your hands do. We just try to give it good things to remember.”— Mahnoor Rafiq, founder
