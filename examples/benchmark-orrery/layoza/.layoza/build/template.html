    Rooftop telescopes on clear Fridays

    Up on the roof above the dome, with our telescopes pointed at whatever is best that night: the Moon, Saturn, a double star, a cluster.

    1. 19:30

      Meet in the dome

      A short live tour of tonight’s sky, so you know what you are about to look at.

    2. 20:00

      Up to the roof

      Our team sets up four telescopes on the harbour-side roof. Warm coats help; we lend red torches.

    3. 21:30

      Last look, then home

      Take a printed chart of what you saw. Under-16s come with an adult.

    If it’s cloudy

    We check the forecast at 16:00 on the day and text everyone booked. If the sky is lost, the evening moves inside for a longer live dome show, and you can swap to another Friday or take a full refund.

    Questions before you come

    Anything else, call the desk on 0117 496 0742.

    Can we come in if we’re late for a show?

    Yes, for the first ten minutes. The dome is dark, so a member of the team will walk you to seats near the door with a red torch.

    Which show suits which age?

    Little Bear’s First Night is made for ages 3 to 7. The Sky Over the Harbour works from 6 up, Saturn’s Long Year from 8, and Where Stars Are Born from 11. Adults are welcome at all of them.

    Is the dome accessible?

    The dome is on the ground floor with step-free access and space for two wheelchairs; tell us when you book. The roof for telescope nights is reached by a lift.

    What happens if a telescope night is cloudy?

    We decide at 16:00 and text you. The evening becomes a longer live dome show, and you can move to another Friday or have your money back.

    How do we get to you?

    We are on the harbourside, a short walk from the city centre along the water. The harbour ferry stops nearby, and there are bike racks outside the door.
