    Rooftop telescope nights

    Clear Friday? We'll be on the roof.

    On clear Friday nights we wheel our telescopes onto the roof above the dome and point them at whatever is best that evening. £12 per person, £8 for under-16s.

    1. Book a Friday

      Choose one of the next dates below. Places are capped at 24 so everyone gets real time at the eyepiece.

    2. We check the sky at 4pm

      Our team checks cloud cover on the afternoon of the session and emails every guest by 16:30 to confirm it's on.

    3. Meet at the dome door

      Arrive for 20:30. A short indoor briefing, then up to the roof for about 90 minutes of observing with our presenters.

    If it's cloudy

    The evening moves indoors: a live "what you would have seen" show in the dome, using that night's real sky. You can stay for it, or swap to any other Friday at no charge. If you'd rather not, we refund in full.
