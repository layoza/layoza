Rooftop telescope nights

Clear Friday? Meet us on the roof.

On clear Friday evenings we set up telescopes on the rooftop above the dome, looking out over the harbour.

  1. Book a place

    Places are limited so everyone gets proper time at the eyepiece. Tickets are £12, members per their tier.

  2. We check the sky at 4pm

    Our team looks at the forecast and the sky itself, then emails every booking with a go or no-go.

  3. Arrive after sunset

    Meet in the foyer. A presenter takes you up, explains the kit, and points the telescopes at whatever is best that night.

  4. Wrap up warm

    The roof is exposed to the wind off the water. Bring layers, a hat and gloves, even in summer.

If it’s cloudy

If we call it off at 4pm, you can move to any future telescope night or take a seat at the 19:30 dome show that evening instead, at no extra cost. If neither suits, we’ll refund you in full.

Before you visit

Yes, with the age guidance on each show as a guide. The dome gets fully dark for part of every show, and presenters will raise the lights a little if anyone gets unsettled. Under-12s must be with an adult.

The dome and shop are step-free with two wheelchair spaces in each show; mention it when you book so we can hold them. The rooftop is reached by lift. Assistance dogs are welcome everywhere.

Once the dome darkens we close the doors so everyone’s eyes can adjust. If you miss the start, we’ll move you to a later session that day where seats allow.

Weekday morning sessions are reserved for schools. Email us with your year group and topic, and a presenter will shape the session around what your class is studying.

Unused items can be returned within 30 days with proof of purchase. If a telescope isn’t working the way you hoped, bring it in and we’ll help you set it up first.
