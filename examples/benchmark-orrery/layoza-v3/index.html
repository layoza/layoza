    Clear Friday? Come up on the roof.

    On clear Friday nights the roof becomes a small observatory: three telescopes, red torches, and a guide who knows where everything is.

    1. 20:00

      Meet in the foyer. Eyes start adjusting while we hand out red torches and a star chart.

    2. 20:20

      Up on the roof. A ten-minute tour of the sky with a pointer, naked eye first.

    3. 20:30–22:00

      At the eyepieces: the Moon, a planet when one is up, double stars and clusters.

    If it is cloudy

    We decide by 16:00 on the day and email everyone booked. A cloudy night moves into the dome for a live “what you would have seen” show, or you can switch to any other Friday at no charge.

    Before you come.

    Anything else, ask the box office. A person answers, usually one of the presenters.

    0117 496 0123

    How early should we arrive?
    Fifteen minutes before the show. The doors close when the lights go down, because opening them lets light into the dome.
    Is the dome suitable for young children?
    Small Wanderers is made for ages three to seven. The dome is never fully dark during that show, and you can step out at any time.
    Is there step-free access?
    Yes. The foyer, dome and shop are on one level, with two wheelchair spaces in the dome. Tell us when you book and we keep them free. The roof is reached by a lift.
    What happens if a telescope night is cloudy?
    We decide by 16:00 and email you. You can join a live dome show of that night’s sky instead, or move to any other Friday at no charge.
    Can I bring my own telescope or binoculars?
    Please do. Bring it on a Friday and a guide will help you set it up and find your first targets.
