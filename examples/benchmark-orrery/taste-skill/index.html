      Before you come

      3 Cannon Quay, Harbourside, Bristol.

      Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–21:00.

      A short walk from the harbour ferry landings.

      Can I bring a child under five?

      Yes. Little Dippers is made for ages 3 to 7 and keeps the lights half up. Under-twos sit on a lap, free, in any show.

      What happens on a cloudy Friday?

      We decide by noon and email everyone booked. If it is cloudy the evening moves into the dome for a live tour of the sky you would have seen, or you can move your place to another Friday at no cost.

      Is the dome step-free?

      Yes. There is level entry from the quay, a lift to the roof, and four wheelchair spaces in the dome. Tell us when you book and we will keep them clear.

      What if I arrive late?

      The doors close when the lights go down, so eyes that have adjusted to the dark are not dazzled. Come fifteen minutes early; if you miss the start we will move you to the next session with seats.

      Do members still need to book?

      Yes, because there are only sixty seats. Members use the same booking form, and the tickets included in their membership come off the price at the desk.
