# Brief: Orrery (fictional)

Landing page for **Orrery**, a small independent planetarium and night-sky shop in Bristol's harbourside. A 60-seat dome runs live, presenter-led shows, with school sessions on weekdays. On clear Friday nights there is a rooftop telescope evening. A small shop sells star maps, telescopes and kits.

Required content:
1. Header: logo/wordmark, nav (Shows, Telescope nights, Shop, Schools, Visit), basket button with item count.
2. Hero with a clear headline, supporting line, and primary + secondary call to action.
3. Shows: 4 dome shows (title, what it's about, duration, age guidance, price in GBP).
4. Show booking form: name, email, show select, date (next 14 days), session time (11:00, 14:00, 19:30), number of tickets, submit with inline validation and a success message.
5. Shop: 6 items (name, short description, GBP price, "Add to basket" button that updates the basket count).
6. Membership: 3 tiers (yearly price, perks) with a "Join" button each.
7. Telescope nights: how it works, what happens if it is cloudy, next 3 dates.
8. 3 visitor testimonials.
9. FAQ accordion (5 questions).
10. Footer with address, opening hours, phone, social links.

Constraints:
- One `index.html` with inline CSS and JS. Google Fonts allowed. Pinned-version JavaScript libraries from cdn.jsdelivr.net are allowed (e.g. an animation library). No external images: use CSS/SVG/canvas artwork.
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (basket count, form validation, accordion, mobile navigation).
