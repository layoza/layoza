        Friday nights on the roof

        When the sky is clear, the rooftop opens after the last show and our telescopes point wherever the best view is that night.

        1. Reserve a place

          Up to 24 people a night, £12 each, members first. Children 8 and over with an adult.

        2. We read the sky at 16:00

          On the day, our presenter checks cloud cover and calls it. You get a text and an email either way.

        3. Up to the roof at 20:30

          Warm layers on. Two hours at the eyepieces with a guide: the Moon, planets, double stars, a galaxy if the night allows.

        If it's cloudy

        We call the night off by 16:00, never at the door. Your place moves to the next clear Friday, or we refund you in full. Your choice, no forms.
