## Direction contract

THESIS: Orrery's page is its printed season programme: a two-ink mid-century planetarium bill where the 60-seat dome is drawn as a working diagram. It refuses the category default of a navy starfield with glowing gradients and a planet render.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated cool-grey stock (#E9ECEF), ultramarine ink (#1F2BB8) owning whole fields, sodium amber ink (#FFB000) reserved for what is active or selected; overprint where they meet (multiply). Archivo at many widths and weights as the only face; hierarchy by size alone. Ruled timetable rows, halftone dot screens, square-cornered tickets with perforation.

STORY: Visitor sees a live, presenter-led show in a small dome, understands sessions, prices and ages at a glance, books in place, then browses shop, membership and Friday rooftop nights, honest about cloud.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7 columns: wide-black Archivo headline at ~5.5rem, one supporting line, Book a show (amber ink on ultramarine) and Telescope nights. Right 5 columns: the dome elevation diagram in ultramarine, 60 seat dots, presenter console, slowly turning projected sky. Below the fold edge: today's timetable rule with three sessions.

FORM: candidate 3 of 7, mid-century planetarium programme. seed: none (unavailable)
