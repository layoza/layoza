    Under the dome, a person tells you about tonight's sky.

    Orrery is a 60-seat planetarium on Bristol's harbourside. Every show is presented live, so you can ask questions as the stars turn overhead.

    Book a dome show See telescope Fridays

    Shows daily at 11:00, 14:00 and 19:30. School sessions on weekday mornings.

    What's on in the dome

    Four shows run in rotation. A presenter steers the projector live, so each one changes a little with the season and the questions in the room.

      School sessions

      Weekday mornings are kept for schools, from Reception to Year 9. Sessions follow the national curriculum for Earth and space, last 50 minutes and cost £6 per pupil, with staff free. Email schools@orrery.example to hold a date.

      Telescope nights on the roof

      On clear Friday evenings we open the roof terrace above the dome and point four telescopes at whatever is up: the Moon, a planet, a double star.

      1. Book a place

        Twenty places a night, £12 each, £8 for members. Children under 16 come with an adult.

      2. Meet us in the foyer

        We start about an hour after sunset, so the time moves through the year. Your ticket email gives the exact time on the Wednesday before.

      3. Go up to the roof

        A presenter shows you how to find each target, then you take turns at the eyepieces. Allow 90 minutes, and bring a warm layer.

      If it's cloudy

      We check the forecast at 4pm on the day. If the sky won't clear, we text you and you choose: a free seat at the 19:30 dome show that night, a place on another Friday, or a full refund.

      Next three Fridays

        Moon shown as it will look on each night. A bright Moon is lovely to look at but washes out faint galaxies.

        Questions

        Anything else, call the desk on 0117 496 0412.

        What age are the shows suitable for?

        Each show lists a guide age. The dome goes fully dark and the seats recline, so very young children sometimes find it a lot; the 11:00 shows keep the lights at a low glow for them. Under-12s come with an adult.

        Is the dome accessible?

        Yes. There is step-free access from the quay, two wheelchair spaces in the dome and a hearing loop. Tell us when you book and we will keep the spaces for you. Carers come free.

        Can I arrive late?

        Please arrive 15 minutes early. Once the dome is dark we can only seat latecomers at the next natural pause, usually around ten minutes in.

        How do I know if a telescope night is going ahead?

        We decide at 4pm on the Friday and text everyone booked. If it is cancelled, you choose a free dome seat that night, another Friday, or a refund.

        Can I return something from the shop?

        Unused items can come back within 30 days with a receipt for a refund. If a telescope or kit arrives faulty, bring it in and we will swap it.
