Who catches it

Marnie Hale grew up picking crab on her grandfather’s boat. She came home from London kitchens to cook only what lands within sight of the door.

Four boats and one farm supply the kitchen. If the weather keeps the boats in, the dish comes off the board and we tell you why.

The boats we buy from

  • Grey HeronNed Farrow. Brown crab and lobster, potted off Scolt Head.
  • KittiwakeSally Crane. Bass and mackerel on hand lines, May to October.
  • Two SistersThe Dunnett family. Mussels and oysters from the harbour lays.
  • Morning StarJoe Pell. Plaice, dab and skate from a small inshore trawl.
  • Lane End FarmRuth Okafor. Vegetables and eggs from two miles inland.
