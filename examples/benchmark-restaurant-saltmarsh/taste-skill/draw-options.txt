# 3 directions dealt for this brief

Choose the one whose way of composing can best carry an idea from your inventory (SKILL.md steps 1 and 4), not the
most familiar one. Then deal it in full with the same command plus `--direction <id>` (and without --options).

## `dark-room` — The dark room
Idea engine: One dark field from top to bottom, and in it, lit things. Photographs and objects appear like exhibits in a dim gallery: each with space around it, each the brightest thing on its screen.
Composition: A single near-black or deep-toned ground runs the full page with no section bands. First screen: either the name at full width in a light weight along the bottom edge with one dim photograph above it, or one object or photograph centred at 40–50% of the viewport with a line of text beneath at 16–18px.

## `place-carries-it` — The place carries it
Idea engine: For a place, the photograph is the argument. One full-bleed image of where you would be standing fills the first screen, and the design steps back: the name sits along the bottom edge like a film title, or small in a corner, and nothing else interrupts the view.
Composition: First screen: a single photograph at 100vw by 100svh, focal point kept clear of text. The name is set either very large (14–20vw) hugging the bottom edge with 16–24px of margin, or small (20–28px) top-left with a one-line description bottom-left.
Needs: many pictures. Without photographs of its own, each is a real picture of the subject's world found with scripts/assets.mjs, or a drawing you make.

## `uneven-photographs` — Photographs at uneven sizes
Idea engine: A calm page where the pictures are placed like prints on a gallery wall: each at its own size and position, with text set small and light between them. Nothing lines up in the obvious way, yet everything sits on a grid you can feel.
Composition: A 12-column grid with wide margins (5–7vw). The hero is a single line of large light type (6–9vw) across the top third, and below it one photograph placed off-centre (columns 6–12, 16:10), its top edge overlapping the baseline zone of the headline by a few pixels of deliberate tension.
Needs: many pictures. Without photographs of its own, each is a real picture of the subject's world found with scripts/assets.mjs, or a drawing you make.
