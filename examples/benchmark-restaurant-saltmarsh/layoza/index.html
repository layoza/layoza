Four boats and one kitchen

Nell Ashby buys straight off the hard at Wrayham Staithe. No market, no lorry, no menu until the boats are in.

Nell grew up two creeks along, picking samphire for pocket money and gutting mackerel on her uncle's boat. She cooked in Bristol and London for twelve years before coming home in 2019 to take on the derelict lifeboat shed that is now Saltmarsh.

The kitchen works to the tide table. On a good morning the crab comes in still snapping at half six and is on the lunch menu by noon. When the wind is up and the boats stay on the hard, the menu gets shorter, and we say so.

Everything that isn't fish comes from within twenty miles: potatoes from the Brook farm, butter from Crane Dairy, bread from our own oven.

“If the boats don't go out, neither does the crab.”

Nell Ashby, chef and owner
