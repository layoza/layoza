REVIEW AGAINST BRIEF / DEFAULTS
- "Boat register list with numbers" risks the numbered-marker tell. Kept: registrations are real identifiers for boats, not 01/02 decoration.
- All-caps hull lettering on the hero only (it is the painted name, subject vernacular); no all-caps labels anywhere else. Changed from caps section headings to sentence case.
- Hairline rules for menu rows would drift into the broadsheet default. Changed: menu rows separated by space and a plank-tone band per section.
- Lavender accent instead of red for actions: red reserved for the waterline band, so the accent appears only on actions and selected states.
- Motion: one load moment only (water rises to the waterline, then the registration is painted in). No per-section fade-ups.
