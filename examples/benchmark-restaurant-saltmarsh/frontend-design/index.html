KL 40

Forty covers in the old boathouse at Thornham Staithe. We cook what the day boats land, and the menu changes when they do.

KL 40 is our number: forty seats, like a boat registered at King’s Lynn.Open Tuesday to Sunday. Closed Mondays.

Tamsin Rook, chef and owner, on the staithe at low water.

Cooked a few yards from where it lands

Tamsin grew up three doors from the boathouse. After ten years cooking in Norwich and London she came back, bought the shed her grandfather mended nets in, and built a kitchen where the slipway used to be.

Each morning she walks down to the staithe and buys from four boats she has known all her life. If the weather keeps them in, the dish comes off the board. Vegetables come from one farm two miles inland.
