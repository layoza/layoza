Cooked by the harbour, for the harbour

Marnie Hale learned to pick crab on her grandfather's boat before she learned to cook. Saltmarsh is her answer to the question she kept being asked in London kitchens: why don't we just cook what's here?

The menu is written each morning once the boats are in. When the weather keeps them ashore, the dishes change. Everything else, from the bread to the butter, is made on site.

The boats and growers we buy from

  • Grey Heron (BH 7)

    Ned Farrow — crab and lobster from pots off Scolt Point.

  • Kittiwake (BH 12)

    Sally Crane — line-caught bass and mackerel, May to October.

  • Two Sisters (BH 3)

    The Dunnett family — mussels and oysters from the harbour lays.

  • Lane End Farm

    Ruth Okafor — vegetables, herbs and eggs, two miles inland.
