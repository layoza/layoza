A kitchen that waits for the boats

There is no fish order. Chef Tamsin Garrow walks down to the staithe at first light, sees what came in, and writes the board from that.

Tamsin cooked in Bristol and Copenhagen before coming home to the coast in 2019. Her food is plain on purpose: crab on dripping toast, sole on the bone, samphire picked off the marsh you can see from your table. If the boats stay in because of weather, the menu gets shorter and nobody pretends otherwise.

Tamsin GarrowChef and co-owner, with front-of-house partner Owen Lyle
