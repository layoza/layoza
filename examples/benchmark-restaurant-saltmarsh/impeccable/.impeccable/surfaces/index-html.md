## Direction contract

THESIS: Saltmarsh is printed as the harbour's pocket tide-table booklet: the catch follows the tide, so the site is a table of times. Refuses the cream-and-serif candlelit restaurant hero with a photo and a "Book now" button.
OWN-WORLD: Chandlery booklet. Cover in saturated chart yellow (#F2C230) with deep sea-blue ink (#0E2F4F) and one oxide red for warnings/closed; interior pages cool newsprint grey-white (#EEF1F0), hard 1px blue rules, tabular figures everywhere, condensed grotesque headings (Barlow Condensed) with a workhorse text face (Public Sans). Yellow is law: it marks open/available only. Booklet ad-panels hold vouchers and private dining.
STORY: Visitor sees today's tide and the next open tables, understands the menu follows the boats, picks a slot, books, then browses the landings, press, the loft and the map.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/12: yellow cover block, SALTMARSH masthead, "Tides & Tables" kicker, one-line hook, address. Right 7/12: live tide curve for today with a NOW marker, and beneath it a printed table of the next 6 service days with open sitting times as yellow cells (closed Mondays struck in red); clicking a cell carries it into the booking form.
FORM: harbour pocket tide-table booklet, position 3 of 7. seed: none (unavailable)
Raises: single line weight on all drawn art (from ligne claire); dense ruled date cells with struck past/closed days (from angura poster); reserved-colour law, yellow = open (from CRT arcade); labels pinned to figures, tabular digits (from event display); each dish seamed to its boat (from memory quilt).
Signature interaction: tide curve + slot cells drive the booking form.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
