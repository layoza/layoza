## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: single self-contained static HTML/CSS/JS file (brief requires it).

## Users

Visitors to the Norfolk coast, weekend travellers from London and Norwich, locals marking an occasion, groups looking for a private room, and people buying a gift. Job: decide whether to eat here, check what is cooking, and book a table (or enquire / buy a voucher). Often on a phone, often already on the coast (inferred).

## Product Purpose

Saltmarsh is a 40-cover seasonal seafood restaurant in a converted boathouse on the Norfolk coast. The menu changes with the season and with what local day boats land. Success: a visitor books a table for an open slot without friction.

## Positioning

The menu is set by what the day boats land, cooked forty seats from where they land it. A neighbouring restaurant cannot truthfully copy the boathouse or the named boats.

## Operating Context

Tides, day boats, landings, a small dining room, lunch and dinner services, closed Mondays.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Menu tabs Lunch / Dinner / Drinks with V, VG, GF tags and a working filter. Booking: date, slot (only open slots), party 1–8, name, email, validation, confirmation. Private dining enquiry. Opening hours and drawn map. Gift vouchers. Press quotes. Footer. Prices in GBP.

## Evidence on Hand

None. All people, boats, publications and quotes are fictional and must be labelled as such; no real reviews or trademarks.

## Product Principles

1. The catch decides; the site shows what landed.
2. Booking is the action; never more than one scroll from it.
3. Small and specific beats grand and generic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

ARIA tabs pattern, announced form errors, keyboard reachable, visible focus, reduced motion respected, no horizontal scroll at 390px.
