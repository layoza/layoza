Who landed it

Four boats and one farm. When the weather keeps the boats in, the menu changes.

Marnie came home from London kitchens with one question: why not just cook what is here? Saltmarsh is the answer, written each morning once the boats are in.

Bread, butter and ice cream are made on site. Fish is bought whole and cooked whole where it can be. If a boat lands nothing, the dish comes off the board and we tell you why.
