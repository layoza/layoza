Bought off
the boats

Three boats, one creek, one cook. If a boat stays in, the dish comes off the board. That is the whole system.

I write the menu at seven, once I know what Danny has in the hold. Nothing here is decided further ahead than the tide.

Iris CallowayChef and owner, born two villages along

Iris cooked for a decade in London before buying the derelict lifeboat shed on the staithe in 2019. She kept the tarred boards, the slipway and the winch, and put forty seats where the boat used to be. Upstairs, the old net loft is now a room for private parties.

Fish arrives at the back door between six and nine, still cold from the hold. What isn't cooked by Sunday goes to the smoker or into the stock.
