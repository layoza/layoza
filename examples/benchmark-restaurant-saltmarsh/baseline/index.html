Saltmarsh is a forty-cover restaurant at Holmesby Staithe on the north Norfolk coast. Our menu follows the tides, the season and whatever comes in on the morning boats.

Meet the kitchen

Chef Tamsin Rook cooks what the boats bring in, and very little else.

Tamsin grew up in a fishing family a few miles along the coast and spent a decade cooking in Norwich and London before returning to open Saltmarsh in 2020. The menu is written every morning once the catch is in, alongside vegetables from two farms within ten miles of the door.

If the weather keeps the boats ashore, the menu gets shorter and more vegetable-led. We'd rather tell you that than buy from far away.
