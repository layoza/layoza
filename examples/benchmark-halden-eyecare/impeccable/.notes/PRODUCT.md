## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated (by brief): one self-contained static `index.html`, inline CSS and JS, Google Fonts allowed, no external images (CSS/SVG artwork only).

## Users

Inferred: people living or working around Leith, Edinburgh who need an eye test (NHS, free in Scotland), want new frames, or need a frame fixed. Situation: deciding whether to book with an independent optician instead of a high-street chain.

## Product Purpose

Halden Eyecare is an independent optician on the Shore in Leith, Edinburgh. Success for the page: visitors book an appointment, add frames to the bag, or choose a lens package.

## Positioning

Two optometrists (Dr. Isla Fraser, Dr. Tomasz Nowak), a small on-site workshop that fits and repairs frames, and a curated wall of frames from small European makers. Independence plus on-site making/repair is the mechanism a chain cannot truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

NHS eye tests are free in Scotland; private extended tests and contact-lens fittings are paid. Appointments by optometrist, day (next 14 days), and time slot (morning/afternoon/evening).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Required content: header (wordmark, nav Eye tests/Frames/Lenses/About/Visit, bag with count), hero with primary + secondary CTA, 4 appointment types, booking form with inline validation + success message, 6 frames with Add to bag, 3 lens packages with Choose, workshop services with turnaround times, 3 testimonials, 5-question FAQ accordion, footer with address, hours, phone, social links. Must work at 1440x900, 768x1024, 375x812.

OPEN: exact prices, durations, turnaround times, address number, phone, hours, frame names and makers are not given in the brief; the page authors illustrative values and they must be replaced with real ones.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Halden Eyecare". No other assets given.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real testimonials, awards, or press exist. Testimonials on the page are illustrative and must be labelled/replaced; no awards or third-party claims may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Free NHS test is the honest front door; paid extras are clearly priced.
2. Show the hands: the workshop is the differentiator.
3. Local and specific (the Shore, Leith) over generic optical-retail gloss.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Eye-care audience includes people with low vision: WCAG 2.2 AA minimum, generous type size, strong contrast, works with zoom and keyboard.
