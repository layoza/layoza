    Questions we hear at the counter

    Anything else, call us on 0131 496 0381 or ask in the shop.

    Is my eye test really free?

    Yes. NHS eye examinations are free for everyone in Scotland. How often you can have one depends on your age and eye health, and we will tell you when you are next due. Extended tests and contact lens fittings are private and paid.

    Do I have to buy glasses from you after my test?

    No. Your prescription is yours and we hand it to you after every test. You are welcome to browse the frame wall, but there is no obligation.

    Can you repair frames I bought somewhere else?

    Yes. Our workshop adjusts, repairs and re-glazes frames from anywhere. Bring them in and we will tell you what is possible and how long it will take before we start.

    How long do new glasses take?

    Most single vision glasses are ready in about a week. Varifocals and high-index lenses can take a little longer. We call or email as soon as your pair is fitted and checked.

    Do you see children?

    Yes. Children's eye examinations are free on the NHS. We use picture charts for younger children and keep the appointment relaxed. Book the children's eye examination in the form above.
