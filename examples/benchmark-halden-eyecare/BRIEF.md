# Brief: Halden Eyecare (fictional)

Landing page for **Halden Eyecare**, an independent optician on the Shore in Leith, Edinburgh. Two optometrists, a small workshop that fits and repairs frames on site, and a curated wall of frames from small European makers. NHS eye tests are free in Scotland; private extended tests and contact-lens fittings are paid.

Required content:
1. Header: logo/wordmark, nav (Eye tests, Frames, Lenses, About, Visit), bag button with item count.
2. Hero with a clear headline, supporting line, and primary + secondary call to action.
3. Eye tests: 4 appointment types (name, what's included, price in GBP or "Free (NHS)", duration).
4. Appointment booking form: name, email, appointment type select, optometrist select (Dr. Isla Fraser, Dr. Tomasz Nowak, no preference), preferred day (next 14 days), time slot (morning/afternoon/evening), submit with inline validation and a success message.
5. Frames shop: 6 frames (name, maker and material, GBP price, "Add to bag" button that updates the bag count).
6. Lenses: 3 lens packages (price, what's included) with a "Choose" button each.
7. Workshop: what the on-site workshop does (adjustments, repairs, re-glazing) with turnaround times.
8. 3 patient testimonials.
9. FAQ accordion (5 questions).
10. Footer with address, opening hours, phone, social links.

Constraints:
- One self-contained `index.html` (inline CSS and JS). Google Fonts allowed. No external images: use CSS/SVG artwork.
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (bag count, form validation, accordion, mobile navigation).
