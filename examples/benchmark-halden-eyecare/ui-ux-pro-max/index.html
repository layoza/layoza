    The workshop

    Fitted and fixed behind the counter

    Halden is an independent optician with a small workshop at the back of the shop. Frames are fitted, adjusted, repaired and re-glazed here, not sent away.

    Bring them in bent, loose or scratched. Most things we can sort while you wait.

    1. Adjustments

      Re-shaping arms and nose pads so frames sit level and stop slipping. Free, whoever made them.

      10 minWhile you wait

    2. Repairs

      New screws, hinges and nose pads, and soldering of metal frames where the joint allows it.

      Same dayMost repairs

    3. Re-glazing

      New lenses cut and fitted into the frames you already love, with your current prescription.

      5–7 daysTurnaround

    4. New frame fitting

      Every new pair is fitted to your face at collection, with a free re-check whenever you need one.

      20 minAt collection
