Eye tests

Every test is with one of our two optometrists, not a technician. If you live in Scotland, the NHS covers a full eye examination, so the standard test costs you nothing.

Appointment types
AppointmentWhat's includedPriceTime
NHS eye examination Vision and prescription check, eye pressure, a look at the front and back of the eye, and advice on glasses or lenses. Free (NHS) 30 min
Extended eye health check Everything in the NHS exam plus an OCT scan and retinal photographs, kept on file so we can compare year to year. £65 45 min
Contact lens fitting Measurements, trial lenses, a lesson in putting them in and taking them out, and a follow-up a week later. £50 40 min
Contact lens check-up Comfort and fit check for current wearers, with an updated lens prescription if you need one. £30 20 min

The workshop at the back

Behind the shop is a bench with a lens edger, a frame heater and a drawer of hinges, screws and nose pads. Most small jobs we can do while you wait, whoever's glasses they are.

Halden is run by Dr. Isla Fraser and Dr. Tomasz Nowak, the two optometrists who do every test. The frames and the repairs are theirs too.
