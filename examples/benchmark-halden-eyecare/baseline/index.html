About · The workshop

Fixed at the back of the shop, not sent away.

Halden is an independent practice run by optometrists Dr. Isla Fraser and Dr. Tomasz Nowak. Our small workshop fits, adjusts and repairs frames on site — including frames you didn't buy from us.

  • Adjustments

    Slipping, pinching or sitting crooked? We'll reshape temples, nose pads and bridge fit while you wait.

    TurnaroundWhile you wait

  • Repairs

    Replacement screws, hinges, nose pads and temple tips; soldering for many metal frames.

    TurnaroundSame day – 3 days

  • Re-glazing

    New lenses cut and fitted into your existing frames, after a check that the frame will take them.

    Turnaround5–7 working days
