        A practice with a workshop in the back

        Halden is an independent optician on the Shore in Leith: two optometrists, one frame wall and a bench where frames are fitted, adjusted and repaired on site.

        Because the bench is a few steps from the testing room, small jobs rarely leave the building. Bring in a pair you bought anywhere and we will look at it.
