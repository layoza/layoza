    Four ways to sit in the chair

    Every appointment is with one of our two optometrists, in the same room, with the same care. Pick the one that fits and book it below.

    NHS eye examinations are free for everyone in Scotland. Extended checks and contact-lens fittings are private and priced here in full.

    • NHS eye examination

      Vision and prescription, a health check of the front and back of the eye, and eye pressure where it is needed.

      Takes 30 min

      Free (NHS)

    • Extended eye health

      Everything in the NHS exam, plus a 3D OCT scan of the retina and widefield photographs, explained on screen.

      Takes 45 min

      £45

    • Contact-lens fitting

      Measurements, trial lenses to take home, a handling lesson, and a follow-up check two weeks later.

      Takes 45 min

      £60

    • Dry-eye clinic

      Tear-film and eyelid assessment under magnification, and a care plan you can follow at home.

      Takes 40 min

      £40

    The workshop behind the counter

    Loose hinge, lost screw, a frame you love with an old prescription. It is mended here, not posted away.

    • Adjustments and nose pads
