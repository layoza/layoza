    Our story

    R

    We are Ruth and Owen, and Loom & Leaf is the two of us in two rooms above a tyre shop in Colne, with Pendle Hill in the back window.

    Ruth weaves. Owen cuts, hems and sews. Every piece passes through both pairs of hands, and the one who finished it sews their initial into the seam.

    We weave in runs of forty metres, so a colour lasts until the run is gone. When something sells out it may come back a shade different, and we think that is how linen ought to be.

    Our cloth is a mid-weight linen, washed twice so it arrives soft and only gets softer. Wash it at forty, hang it on the line, iron it damp if you like.

    If anything is not right, you will be writing to one of us.

    Delivery and returns

    How much is delivery? £4.50 by tracked post anywhere in the UK, and free when your basket reaches £60.

    When will it arrive? We post from Colne on Tuesdays and Fridays, and tracked post takes two to four working days after that.

    Do you post abroad? Not yet. We are two people and the post office queue is long enough already.

    Can I return something? Yes, within 30 days if it is unwashed and unused. Write to us first and we will send a prepaid label.

    How quickly is the refund? Within three working days of the parcel reaching us, to the card you paid with.

    What if a hem comes loose? Post it back at any age and Owen will mend it for free. We would rather mend than replace.

    Can you send it as a gift? Yes. Tell us at checkout and we leave out the receipt and write a card by hand.

    Will the colour match the screen? Close, not exact. Linen dyed in small runs varies a little; ask and we will post a cutting.

    How do I wash it? At forty degrees, line dry, warm iron while damp. No softener; linen softens itself.
