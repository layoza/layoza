    Two pairs of hands, one weaving shed

    Ruth weaves; Owen cuts, hems and sews. We work from a single room in an old Pennine-edge weaving shed, where the light comes in through the north-facing roof that was built for looms.

    Our cloth is a mid-weight linen, washed twice so it arrives soft. We weave in runs of forty metres, which is why colours come and go with the seasons, and why a sold-out piece may not return exactly the same.

    Every item carries a woven label with the initial of whoever finished it. If something isn't right, you write to one of the two people who made it.

    Loom & Leaf, Ruth and Owen are fictional, created for a design demonstration.

    Delivery & returns

    UK delivery

    £4.50 by tracked post, 2–4 working days. Free on orders of £60 or more. We post from the studio on Tuesdays and Fridays.

    30-day returns

    Unwashed and unused? Send it back within 30 days for a full refund. Write to us first and we'll send a prepaid label.

    Repairs, always

    A loose hem or a pulled thread, any time: post it back and Owen will mend it free. Gift notes are handwritten on request.
