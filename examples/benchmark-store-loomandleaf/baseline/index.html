    Our story

    Two people, one small studio, a lot of linen

    Loom & Leaf began at a kitchen table in Lancashire, when Hannah and Joe (fictional founders for this demo) wanted tea towels that would actually last. Today they work from a converted barn, cutting and sewing every piece by hand.

    We use stonewashed European flax linen, which gets softer with every wash, and natural plant dyes where we can.

    Cut

    Each piece is measured and cut by hand from the bolt.

    Sew

    Double-turned hems and mitred corners for a long life.

    Wash

    Pre-washed so it arrives soft and won't shrink much.

    Good to know

    Delivery & returns

    Every order is wrapped in recycled tissue and sent from our studio within two working days.

    UK delivery

    Standard delivery is £4.50 and takes 2–4 working days. Orders of £60 or more ship free.

    International shipping

    We currently ship to the EU and Ireland from £9.50. Duties may apply on arrival.

    Returns & exchanges

    Unused, unwashed items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Just email us and we'll send a returns label.

    Caring for linen

    Machine wash at 40°C, line dry, and iron while slightly damp if you like a crisp finish.
