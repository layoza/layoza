The linen

    Two looms, one sewing table, a view of Pendle

    Loom & Leaf is Mara and Joe, in a converted cotton office in Clitheroe. Mara weaves on two floor looms; Joe cuts, hems and packs.

    We dye small batches with weld, madder and woad, so a run of cloth is about forty metres and no two runs match exactly. The linen is washed twice before it is sewn, so it arrives soft and does not shrink in your machine.

    Both of us are fictional, as is the studio. The cloth is how we would make it.

    Delivery and returns

    UK delivery is £4.95 and free on orders of £60 or more. The basket shows how close you are.

    When it arrives
    We post on Tuesdays and Fridays by tracked Royal Mail, two to three working days. You get the tracking number by email.
    Returns
    Send back unused, unwashed pieces within 30 days for a full refund. Email us first and we will send a returns label.
    If something is wrong
    A loose hem or a fault in the weave, at any age: send it back and Joe will mend it, or we will replace it.
    Outside the UK
    We do not post abroad yet.

    Hear when a new run is off the loom

    One email for each new colour, roughly every two months. No other mail.
