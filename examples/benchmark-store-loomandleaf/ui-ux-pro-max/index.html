    Our studio

    Small batches, made slowly

    Loom & Leaf is a fictional two-person studio in a Lancashire mill town. We buy stonewashed linen by the bolt, cut every piece by hand on a long oak table, and sew it on two old machines that refuse to retire.

    Nothing is mass-produced. Each batch is twenty or thirty pieces, so colours come and go with the seasons.

    1. 1. Choose

      We pick heavy, stonewashed linen that softens with every wash.

    2. 2. Cut

      Measured and cut by hand, straight along the grain.

    3. 3. Sew

      Double-turned hems and mitred corners on every piece.

    4. 4. Post

      Wrapped in recycled tissue and sent within two working days.
