How the parts connect

A call, a booking form, a phone in a van and a paid invoice are one system. Pick any part to see what it does and where its limits are.

The board

Every technician is a lane and every job a block. Drag to plan by hand, or let the board place jobs by skill, distance and the hours left in each lane. When a job overruns, it moves the next one and tells you why.

It replans the whole crew in under two seconds and keeps a written log of every change, so the office can undo any move.
