PURPOSE: One-page site for Counterform, a fictional independent brand and digital design studio in Leith. Prospective clients judge the work, then open a case study or start a project.

PICKED: auto — Direction A (Type Architecture, Syne / Plus Jakarta Sans, obsidian-vermilion). A design studio sells letters and judgement, so a page where type is the architecture and the work sits inside the letterforms shows the craft directly. B's scroll sequence and C's light mono bento read as product or dev-tool pages, not a type-led brand studio.

SHAPE: hero pinned-story (hero pins for 22% of the first viewport, fixed in px. The Three.js "counter of an O" is cut into 12 sorts and covers about 45% of the desktop width behind the headline. It rests as an assembled O, and on scroll the sorts lift apart while the headline weight scrubs from 800 to 560) | flow spreads (work: image page left and index right; practice: text left and vermilion ampersand page right; studio: a full-bleed drawn galley proof (inline SVG with red proof marks) and a text plate; method: one huge glyph page left and stepped type right; contact: text left and photo page right) | section heads vertical-label (rotated numbered label along each text page's edge) | ground full-bleed-media (studio galley-proof SVG with parallax, vermilion practice page, WebGL hero) | material typographic (type, rules and numerals carry it; photos are used only as spread image pages, see deviation) | labels numbered in Syne figures (00–05, No. 146, Fig. 1–4) | motion heavy: webgl-scene (Three.js r160, paused off-screen), glyph-stage canvas (Canvas 2D: one letter "a" scrubbed through 4 states, grid, pencil roughs, drawn outline, set vermilion type; this replaces the dealt cursor-field, at the coordinator's request), pinned-scene (GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger), plus a Canvas tile-dissolve work index, ampersand weight scrub, guide rules that draw on, proof marks drawing on, studio parallax, contact wipe (runs once), and a CSS scroll-driven progress bar in the nav island.

DEVIATION: Material is dealt "typographic, no imagery", but the brief's spreads, full-bleed ground and approved photos call for image pages. Photos sit only in the image page of a spread. studio-desks.jpg was dropped because it shows real brand marks. Every heading, label and diagram stays typographic.

ROUND 1: The screenshots showed the headline running off both desktop and mobile, because the hero grid track grew to the nowrap h1's width. Fixed with min-width:0 on the grid children, and replaced the JS fit with CSS calc sizes. Swapped the gradient underlines (QA read them as 1:1 contrast) for pseudo-element lines. Moved the vertical labels after their h2 (they were being read as eyebrows). Rebuilt the nav island: section readout, then numbered tabs, then the wordmark on the right, so it no longer matches the default nav pattern.
ROUND 2: Fixed 28–44px of sideways scroll at 320px (the work grid's min-content, the project titles and the island width). Fixed the step numbers sliding left under the vertical label at tablet (they now enter from the right). Hid the section readout under 900px, where it collided with content. Showed the counter photo as a CSS background to stop decoding a 1600px raster for a 100px slot.
ROUND 3: Enlarged the hero WebGL stage (4/8 split) and re-framed the camera so the exploded sorts no longer clip. Shortened the pin to cut the blank band in full-page stills. Closed the void in the mobile menu. Shrank the "set" glyphs in the cursor field so they stop touching. Made the email break at the @. Fit the footer statement to two lines with no clipping.
ROUND 4: Removed the studio-desks photo (real brands) and drew a galley proof for Studio. Cut lab CLS from 0.348 (tablet) to 0.013 using measured fallback metrics (Syne 142%, Jakarta 106%) and fixed line breaks. Shortened the pin to 22% of the viewport in px, and made the contact wipe and proof marks run once so stills show their end state. Enlarged the ring to about 45vw so it overlaps the headline; it rests assembled and explodes on scroll. Rendered WebGL only on demand and set lagSmoothing(0) so the intro finishes on time. Replaced the letter wallpaper with one glyph in 4 stages tied to the 4 steps. Shortened the headline intro to about 1.1s so captures never catch it half-set.

REVIEWER: 7.5/10. The hero is now a composed stage: a big assembled O behind the slugged headline. Studio and Method each carry one idea. Weakest point: the method glyph shows its rough pencil state in static stills, because the scrub starts mid-way. A band of about 200px of dark pin spacer still sits under the hero in full-page captures.
