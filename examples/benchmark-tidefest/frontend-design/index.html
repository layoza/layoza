Camping and getting there

The site is a short walk from the sand. Here's what to know before you pack.

Camp on the headland

Pitches open Friday 13 August at 14:00 and close Tuesday at 12:00. Bring your own tent; showers and toilets are on the field.

Shuttle from Newquay

Festival shuttles run from Newquay town centre every 20 minutes during opening hours. Your ticket covers the ride.

Driving and parking

Parking is pre-booked only and limited, so share a car if you can. Postcode and directions arrive with your tickets.

Leave no trace

Take everything home, tents included. Refill water for free at the taps around the site and on the beach.

Good to know

Tidefest is 18+. Bring photo ID that shows your date of birth; you'll need it at the gate and at the bars.

It starts around 05:40 on Shoreline each morning and runs for about 90 minutes. Weekend tickets include all three.

There's a raised viewing platform at each stage, accessible toilets, and track matting from the entrance to Shoreline. Email us before you book and we'll plan your visit with you.

Sets go ahead in rain. In high winds we may pause Shoreline and move acts into The Cave. Changes appear here and in our emails.

Tickets can't be refunded, but you can transfer yours to someone else for free up to 7 days before the festival.

Hear it first

One email when set times drop, one when the camping map is out. Nothing else.
