> Inferred from BRIEF.md only. Run was unattended (no answer mechanism); every fact below comes from the written brief, and items marked ASSUMED are inferences.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: single static `index.html` with inline CSS and JS (brief constraint). Google Fonts allowed; pinned jsDelivr libraries allowed; no external images.

## Users

ASSUMED: 18–35 electronic/alternative music fans in the UK deciding whether to attend and which ticket to buy, mostly on phones.

## Product Purpose

Tidefest (fictional): a three-day electronic and alternative music festival on a beach near Newquay, Cornwall, 14–16 August next year. Capacity 8,000. Success: visitor understands what/when/where and buys tickets.

## Positioning

Two stages (Shoreline, The Cave), camping on site, and a sunrise set every morning. The beach + sunrise + sea cave are what a neighbouring festival could not copy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Line-up: 18 fictional artists, Fri/Sat/Sun filter, stage shown.
- Countdown to 14 August next year 12:00 Europe/London.
- Tickets: Day, Weekend, Weekend + camping, GBP, quantity stepper, running total. ASSUMED prices (not given in brief): Day £79, Weekend £179, Weekend + camping £219.
- Camping & getting there (4 blocks), FAQ (5), newsletter with validation.

## Brand Commitments

Client style direction (binding): bold and heavily animated. Motion is the brand: scroll-driven sequences, kinetic type, reactive elements. Must stay fast, accessible, fully usable with reduced motion.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No awards, press, sponsors, attendance history or real artists may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The sea and the sunrise are the product; show them.
2. Ticket purchase is always one tap away.
3. Motion carries the brand but never gates content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; full reduced-motion path.
