Camping & getting there

Pitch up on the clifftop

The camping field opens Thursday at 16:00 and closes Monday at 12:00. Bring your own tent; the walk down to the stages takes about five minutes.

Showers, water points and a quiet zone are on the field.

Train to Newquay

Take the train to Newquay station. A festival shuttle runs from the station to the gate throughout Thursday and Friday.

Check journeys with National Rail before you travel.

Driving and parking

Parking is by pass only, bought with your ticket. Share the car if you can: lanes near the beach are narrow and fill fast.

Drop-off and pick-up have their own lane at the main gate.

Access camping

Level pitches close to the facilities, accessible toilets, and a viewing platform at both stages. Personal assistants come in free with a ticket holder.

Email us before you book so we can plan with you.
