Camp above the bay

The campsite sits on the field above the beach, a short walk down to both stages. Here is how the site fits together and how to reach it.

  1. 1

    The campsite

    Flat pitches on the field above the beach, with showers, water points and a quiet area for early sleepers. Weekend + camping tickets include your pitch.

  2. 2

    Shuttle from Newquay

    Buses run between Newquay and the site entrance from 09:00 on the first day and after the sunrise set each morning. Shuttle times are published with the stage times.

  3. 3

    Driving and parking

    Car parking is inland at the site entrance and limited. Come by shuttle or share a car; there is no parking on the beach.

  4. 4

    Down to the sand

    A marked path leads from the campsite to Shoreline, with a step-free route to a raised viewing platform. Tell us your access needs when you buy.

Before you pack

The questions we hear most. Anything else, write to us and a person will reply.

Tidefest is 18+. Bring photo ID; you will need it at the gate for your wristband.

From 05:00 on Shoreline each morning, running until the sun is up over the bay. Exact times follow the published sunrise for each date.

Weekend and Weekend + camping tickets allow re-entry. Day tickets are single entry.

Swimming is not allowed during the festival. Atlantic currents are strong and the water is closed off after dark.

Both stages are covered and the show goes on in rain. If wind makes the beach unsafe, sets move to The Cave and we post updates here and by email.
