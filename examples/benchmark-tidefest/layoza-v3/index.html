Camping & travel

The beach sits on the Atlantic coast a short ride from Newquay. Here is how the site fits together.

  1. 1

    Camp on the headland

    The campsite is a ten-minute walk above the beach. Pitches open Friday at 16:00, with a quiet field for anyone who wants to be up for the sunrise set.

  2. 2

    Train to Newquay

    Newquay station sits at the end of the branch line. A festival shuttle runs from the station forecourt every 20 minutes on arrival and departure days.

  3. 3

    Driving in

    Follow the A30 into Cornwall, then the yellow Tidefest signs. Car park passes are sold alongside tickets; please fill your car.

  4. 4

    Leave only footprints

    Take your tent home. Bins and recycling sit at every field gate, and the beach is swept clean after each sunrise set.
