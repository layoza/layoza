Info

Five things people ask before their first night on the beach.

Is there an age limit?

Tidefest is 18+. Bring photo ID: you will need it to collect your wristband at the gate.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes. Your wristband is your pass in and out for the days on your ticket. Keep it on until you leave; a cut or lost band can’t be replaced.

What if the weather turns?

The Cave is covered. Shoreline plays through rain, but sets pause if wind makes the rig unsafe. Pack waterproofs and sun cream: it’s the Cornish coast in August.

Is the site accessible?

There is a raised viewing platform at Shoreline, accessible toilets in every block and an accessible camping area close to the gate. Tell us what you need when you book and we will plan it with you.

Do I need a ticket for the sunrise set?

No. Every ticket includes the sunrise set on Shoreline at 05:15. A Day ticket covers the sunrise set at the end of its night.
