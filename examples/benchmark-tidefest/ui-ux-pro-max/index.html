    Camping & getting there

    Pitch up, roll in

    The site sits a short walk from the beach. Here is what you need to know before you pack.

    01

    Cliff-top campsite

    Opens Thursday 16:00, closes Monday 12:00. Bring your own tent; quiet field for light sleepers.

    02

    Train & shuttle

    Take the train to Newquay. Festival shuttles loop from the station to the gate every 20 minutes on all three days.

    03

    Driving & parking

    Car parking must be booked in advance and is limited. Car-share with three or more and your space is half price.

    04

    Access

    Raised viewing platforms at both stages, matting over sand, accessible campsite and a free companion ticket.
