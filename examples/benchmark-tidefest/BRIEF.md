# Brief: Tidefest (fictional) — style: ANIMATED

Landing page for **Tidefest**, a three-day electronic and alternative music festival on a beach near Newquay, Cornwall, 14–16 August next year. Capacity 8,000. Two stages (Shoreline, The Cave), camping, and a sunrise set every morning.

**Style direction from the client (must be followed): bold and heavily animated. Motion is the brand — scroll-driven sequences, kinetic type, reactive elements. It must still be fast, accessible, and fully usable with reduced motion.**

Required content:
1. Header: logo, nav (Line-up, Tickets, Camping, Info), "Buy tickets" button, mobile navigation.
2. Hero with festival name, dates, place and ticket CTA.
3. Line-up: 18 fictional artists across 3 days, with a day filter (Fri, Sat, Sun) and stage shown.
4. Countdown to the opening (14 August next year, 12:00 Europe/London).
5. Tickets: 3 types (Day, Weekend, Weekend + camping) with GBP price, quantity stepper and running total.
6. Camping & getting there: 4 short info blocks.
7. FAQ accordion (5 questions).
8. Newsletter signup: email with validation and success message.
9. Footer with contact, social links.

Constraints:
- One `index.html` with inline CSS and JS. Google Fonts allowed. Pinned-version JavaScript libraries from cdn.jsdelivr.net are allowed. No external images: use CSS/SVG/canvas artwork.
- Must work and look intentional at 1440x900, 768x1024, and 375x812.
- Real, usable interactions (day filter, countdown, ticket stepper/total, accordion, form validation, mobile navigation).
- No invented awards or real-world claims; artists must be fictional.
