    Camping & getting there

    Sleep within earshot of the sea. Here's what you need to know before you pack.

    01

    Clifftop camping

    Campsite opens Thursday 18:00 and closes Monday 12:00. Bring your own tent, or pre-pitched bell tents can be added at checkout.

    02

    By train & shuttle

    Travel to Newquay station, then hop on the festival shuttle bus to the site. Shuttles run every 20 minutes on arrival and departure days.

    03

    Driving & parking

    Parking is limited and must be booked in advance. Car-share if you can: four or more people per car get a reduced parking rate.

    04

    Leave no trace

    Free water refill points across the site and campsite. Take everything home with you, including your tent. The beach was here first.
