# Tidefest build notes (taste-skill run)

## Reading
Event landing page. One job: buy tickets. Style is the client's: bold, heavily animated, motion is the brand,
still fast/accessible/reduced-motion safe. Brief forbids external images: CSS/SVG/canvas only. No assets.mjs, no picsum.

## Inventory
1. The name: TIDE-fest. Tides rise twice a day (~12h25m semidiurnal period).
2. Beach near Newquay, Cornwall; Atlantic swell, headlands, sea stacks, sea caves.
3. 14–16 August 2027, gates 12:00 Europe/London.
4. 8,000 capacity.
5. Two stages: Shoreline (on the sand) and The Cave (in/near the cliff).
6. Sunrise set every morning (05:20-ish, mid-August sunrise in Cornwall is ~06:00; set starts before).
7. Camping on the headland above the beach.
8. Printed things: tide table, festival ticket with stub, wristband, train timetable.
9. Light: navy night sea, pink dawn sky. (Dealt palette navy/pink matches the subject's own colours, kept.)

## Argument
1. Tidefest is three days on a Cornish beach, 14–16 Aug  -> the name, huge; the drawn sea at dawn
2. 18 acts, two stages, a sunrise set each day          -> line-up by day, stage + time on every act
3. The gates open at noon on 14 August                   -> countdown in days..seconds and in tides
4. Three tickets, plain prices                           -> the ticket with stub, steppers, total
5. You can get there, pitch, and wake for sunrise        -> four verb panels
6. Answers to the practical questions                    -> FAQ accordion
7. Hear first about the next release                     -> newsletter
8. Contact                                               -> footer, name at full width

## Direction
Dealt 3: type-only-statement, name-as-monument, loud-flat-poster. Chose name-as-monument (seed 659627944):
the name *is* the subject (tide), so the monument wordmark carries it; a canvas sea can sit in the inset frame.
Redraws: pricing -> pricing-ticket-with-stub (dealt one-sentence-price cannot hold 3 types + stepper; the
ticket is the festival's own artefact). faq -> faq-filled-tile-accordion (dealt blueprint forbids collapsing;
brief requires an accordion). contact dropped (contact lives in footer); newsletter uses a plain field on --surface.

## Idea
On this page, the tide that names Tidefest becomes the water level of the inset sea: it rises as you scroll
the line-up toward the opening, and the countdown is told in high tides as well as days.
Competitor test: another beach festival has a sea but not a name that is the tide; counting down in tides works only here.
Live thing: tide clock countdown (days/hours/min/sec + number of high tides left), plus the sea reacting to the pointer.
