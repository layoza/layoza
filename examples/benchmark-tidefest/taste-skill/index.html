Camping and getting there

Arrive

By train and shuttleTake the train to Newquay. Our shuttle buses leave the station every 20 minutes from 10:00 on Friday and reach the gate in about 15 minutes.

Park

By carParking is booked ahead only, £15 for the weekend. Cars carrying three or more people park nearest the gate; taxis drop off at the north entrance.

Pitch

Camping on the headlandOpen Thursday 16:00 to Monday 12:00. Bring your own tent; water, showers and toilets are on site, with a quiet field for early sleepers.

Rise

The sunrise setAt 05:20 every morning the Shoreline stage plays the sun up. It is a ten-minute walk down from the tents. Bring a layer: the sand is cold.

Good to know

Who can come?

Anyone 12 or over. Under-18s need to come with an adult who holds a ticket, and the campsite is for over-16s only.

What if the weather turns?

The Cave is under cover and the Shoreline stage plays through rain. Sets only stop for high wind, and any change goes on this page and out by email.

Can I swim?

Only between the flags while lifeguards are on duty, 10:00 to 18:00. The sea is closed after dark and during the sunrise sets, whatever the tide is doing.

Can I bring food and drink?

Bring your own food, and sealed soft drinks. No glass anywhere on site, and no alcohol into the arena; there are bars at both stages.

Is the site accessible?

Both stages have a raised viewing platform, there is an accessible camping area by the north gate, and a companion goes free. Write to access@tidefest.example to arrange it.
