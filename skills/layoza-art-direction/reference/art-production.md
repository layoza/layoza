# Material and composition
Follow the canonical process in skills/layoza/references/creative-process.md. The composition decides the medium: real photographs for physical places and objects; a working product view for software; diagrams for relationships; illustration when it contributes an authored idea. Acquire and inspect material before committing to the layout.

Use an intentional focal point, crop and relationship to type. Inspect all supplied alternatives before choosing: an image can fit the industry while working against the audience's emotional need. For an anxious visitor, compare a human conversation with equipment-led imagery instead of accepting the first room photograph. Test the crop on phones; preserve the interaction between people, and show collection works in full unless explicitly presenting a detail. Preserve source, licence, credit and any illustrative-use limitation with design_assets. Never describe a stock model as the actual team or a generated room as the client premises.

Prototype whichever part has the largest creative uncertainty. Complete the whole page before judging rhythm. There is no hero-last rule, fixed picture count, time percentage or section quota. More decoration cannot repair weak hierarchy or generic copy.
