# One creative process

This is the authoritative process for Layoza's design decisions. Style examples,
catalogue directions and older recipes provide material; they do not override it.

1. **Truth.** Establish the actual subject, audience, primary task, required content,
   factual evidence and pinned aesthetic choices. Negative instructions are
   constraints, not evidence of the client's industry. Correct a heuristic misread
   with get_design_system brief_context. Unknowns stay explicit.
2. **Material.** Inventory real assets with design_assets. Obtain or author what
   the concept needs before promising it. Record source, licence, credit, role,
   alt and crop. Never pretend stock photos show the real client or invent proof.
3. **Composition.** For new work/redesigns, render three genuinely different first
   screens with the same content and assets, at desktop and phone sizes. Let each
   demonstrate its hierarchy, type, image relationship and whole-page logic.
   For extensions/improvements, preserve the incumbent world and components.
4. **Choice.** Pick for subject/task fit, expressive strength, clarity and coherent
   execution. Explain gains and sacrifices. Novelty is secondary; familiar
   navigation can be correct. Page shapes, motif IDs and font pools are suggestions.
   Ask for a pick when requested; otherwise choose transparently as the designer.
5. **Build.** Prototype the most uncertain identity-defining composition first,
   then complete the entire page and interactions. No mandatory section count,
   picture quota, world lottery, decorative trick or fixed amount of motion.
   Preserve the native framework and actual project tokens. Use the same 0–10
   motion scale as the engine, with 0 meaning no animation.
6. **Review.** Inspect actual renders for brief fidelity, hierarchy, typography,
   imagery, colour, rhythm, mobile composition and interaction. Name visible
   weaknesses and precise changes. Use an independent blind critic when available;
   label self-review honestly. Default to three review rounds; unresolved material
   issues require further work or a candid incomplete verdict, never a fake pass.
7. **Retain and show.** Use review_design to save candidates, decision, strengths,
   weaknesses, changes and actual screenshot evidence. Record PICKED: auto with
   a reason when selecting yourself. Never fabricate user approval or reviewer
   independence. Show the final designs, evidence and limitations.

Visual, responsive, interaction and accessibility defects still need correction.
Speed diagnostics are optional. A clean checker and a high self-score do not
certify exceptional taste or superiority over other design systems.
