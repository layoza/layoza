# Final visual review

Use [creative-process.md](creative-process.md) and actual desktop/phone renders.

- The first screen communicates the actual subject and primary task.
- Explicit fonts, colours, content and motion choices survived execution.
- One clear hierarchy; supporting detail does not compete with the main idea.
- Type has intentional proportions, line breaks, spacing and contrast.
- Images are relevant, credible, correctly cropped and integrated with the type.
- Sections progress a real argument; the idea and craft reach the footer.
- Phone composition has its own relationships, not a squeezed desktop stack.
- Controls and content remain usable in all important states.
- The strongest two alternatives were considered on the same content/material.
- The final review names visible strengths, weaknesses, changes and evidence.

Novelty, catalogue identifiers and automated cleanliness do not establish the best
design. Familiar patterns can be right. Page shapes and motifs are suggestions.
