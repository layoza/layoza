# Make a design decision
Use the single process in creative-process.md. Understand the real subject and pinned choices; obtain truthful material; render three genuinely different compositions with identical facts and assets. Choose by visible design quality and explain the tradeoff. Ask the user to choose only when requested or when a consequential preference is missing. Record an automatic choice honestly, never as user approval.

Motion uses one 0–10 scale throughout Layoza. Zero means no animation. Do not force novelty, random worlds or a binding motif. Judge actual desktop and phone renders, then persist review_design evidence.
